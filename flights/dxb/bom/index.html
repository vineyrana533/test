Travelling from Dubai to Mumbai: what you need to know

  • Depart from Dubai International Airport (DXB), the city’s main hub with three terminals.
  • Arrive at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM), Mumbai’s sole commercial airport with Terminals 1 and 2.
  • Average non‑stop flight time is about 3 hours 30 minutes.
  • Dubai operates on UTC+4, Mumbai on UTC+5:30 – Mumbai is 1½ hours ahead.
  • The busiest airport‑to‑airport pairing is DXB ↔ BOM, with multiple daily services.
  • For an international departure, aim to be at the departure hall at least two hours before take‑off, more in peak summer or holiday periods.

How to find the cheapest flights from Dubai to Mumbai

  • Book 6‑8 weeks ahead for the best balance of price and seat choice.
  • Historically, October and November deliver the lowest fares, thanks to post‑Ramadan lull and before the winter festival rush.
  • Set a price‑alert on comparison sites; they notify you when the DXB‑BOM market dips.
  • Use the “cheapest” or “fastest” sort filters, then narrow by preferred airlines or lay‑over cities.
  • Consider flying on Tuesdays or Wednesdays and early‑morning slots, which are often cheaper than weekend evenings.

Flying from Dubai: Dubai International Airport

  • Emirates, Air India and IndiGo operate daily non‑stop services from DXB to BOM.
  • Common one‑stop connections use Doha (DOH), Abu Dhabi (AUH), Istanbul (IST) or Singapore (SIN).
  • DXB lies roughly 4.5 km east of Dubai’s historic centre; a car journey takes 15‑20 minutes depending on traffic.
  • The Dubai Metro Red Line reaches Terminal 3 in about 30 minutes from the city centre, with free shuttle buses linking Terminals 1 and 2.

Arriving in Mumbai: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport

  • BOM is situated about 8 km north of South Mumbai; the drive to the city centre typically lasts 25‑35 minutes.
  • A dedicated airport‑city taxi or rides‑hailing app can cover the distance in roughly half an hour, traffic‑dependent.
  • The Mumbai Suburban Railway’s Western Line stops at Andheri (≈10 km from the terminals); a free shuttle bus links the airport to the Andheri station, adding about 15 minutes to the journey.
  • Once in the city, use local trains, auto‑rickshaws, app‑based cabs or the expanding metro network for efficient travel.

Best time to go to Mumbai

  • November is the most popular month, coinciding with festivals such as Diwali and pleasant winter weather.
  • For a quieter, cheaper experience, travel in July or August when the monsoon keeps tourist numbers low.
  • Summer (March‑May) temperatures range from 30 °C to 35 °C, while winter (December‑February) sees milder days between 20 °C and 27 °C, with occasional cool evenings.

More about Mumbai

  • Gateway of India: an iconic arch overlooking the Arabian Sea, the starting point for ferry trips to Elephanta Island.
  • Marine Drive: a sweeping promenade known as the “Queen’s Necklace” for its night‑time lighting.
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus: a UNESCO‑listed Victorian‑Gothic railway station bustling with commuters.
  • Elephanta Caves: rock‑cut temples on an island reachable by a short boat ride from the city harbour.
  • Bandra‑Worli Sea Link: a cable‑stayed bridge offering striking views of the skyline.

Explore more of India

  • New Delhi (≈1 150 km north): the capital’s Red Fort, India Gate and bustling Chandni Chowk market.
  • Jaipur (≈1 150 km north‑west): the Pink City’s Amber Fort, City Palace and vibrant bazaars.
  • Goa (≈960 km south‑west): famous for its golden beaches, Portuguese‑style architecture and lively nightlife.
  • Each city is reachable by a 1‑2 hour flight from Mumbai or a comfortable overnight train, providing diverse cultural experiences beyond the metropolis.

Finding cheap flights from Dubai to Mumbai: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Dubai to Mumbai?

To find cheap flights from Dubai (DXB) to Mumbai (BOM) you should search with flexible dates, use the whole‑month calendar, and set price alerts on this page. Check both Dubai International Airport’s three terminals and consider nearby airports such as Al Maktoum (DWC) for departures. Mid‑week departures, especially Tuesdays and Wednesdays, are usually lower. Booking 6‑8 weeks ahead and monitoring fare drops during sales by Emirates, Air India and low‑cost carriers will maximise savings.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Dubai to Mumbai?

The cheapest way to fly from Dubai to Mumbai is to book a low‑cost carrier such as IndiGo, SpiceJet or Air Arabia on a non‑stop service, travel on a Tuesday or Wednesday, and be flexible with departure times. Use the fare‑comparison tool on this page to view all available options, set a price alert, and book 6‑9 weeks in advance when airlines typically release their lowest inventory for this 1,926‑km route.

When is the best time to book a flight from Dubai to Mumbai to get the lowest price?

The best time to book a flight from Dubai (DXB) to Mumbai (BOM) for the lowest price is 6‑8 weeks before the intended travel date, with an additional dip in fares about 90 days ahead during the low‑season. Monitoring prices on this page and setting a price alert will capture the occasional flash sale from Emirates, Air India or budget airlines. Avoid booking within two weeks of departure, when prices typically rise sharply.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Dubai to Mumbai?

The cheapest month to fly from Dubai to Mumbai is usually May or September, when demand falls between the Indian summer heat and the monsoon season. January and February can also offer lower fares after the holiday peak. During these periods airlines such as Emirates, Air India and IndiGo often release discounted seats, making it the most economical time of year for the 2‑hour 30‑minute flight.

How long is the flight from Dubai to Mumbai?

The non‑stop flight from Dubai International Airport (DXB) to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) typically lasts about 2 hours and 30 minutes, covering a distance of roughly 1,926 kilometres. Flight time can vary by a few minutes depending on wind conditions and the specific aircraft type, but most airlines schedule the journey within this window for both morning and evening departures.

Which airlines fly from Dubai to Mumbai?

Airlines that operate flights between Dubai (DXB) and Mumbai (BOM) include Emirates, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, Air Arabia and GoAir. Emirates offers daily premium and economy services, while Air India provides a mix of full‑service and code‑share options. Low‑cost carriers IndiGo, SpiceJet and Air Arabia run multiple weekly non‑stop flights, giving travellers a range of price points and cabin choices on this popular route.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Dubai to Mumbai?

Yes, there are direct non‑stop flights from Dubai International Airport (DXB) to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) operated by Emirates, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet and Air Arabia. These services run several times a day, with flight times of about 2 hours 30 minutes, eliminating the need for connecting flights and simplifying travel logistics for both business and leisure passengers.

How much does a flight from Dubai to Mumbai usually cost and what drives the price?

A typical economy fare from Dubai to Mumbai ranges from AED 800 to AED 2,200, depending on the airline, booking window and travel season. Prices rise during Indian festivals such as Diwali and during UAE winter holidays, while they fall in the shoulder months of May and September. Low‑cost carriers keep fares at the lower end, whereas full‑service airlines include baggage, meals and lounge access, which increase the overall cost.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

The cheapest day to fly from Dubai to Mumbai is usually Tuesday, followed by Wednesday, with early‑morning departures before 08:00 GMT offering the lowest fares. Mid‑week flights avoid the weekend premium that business travellers pay, and airlines often release discounted inventory for these slots. Checking the fare calendar on this page and selecting these times will typically yield the best price for the 2‑hour 30‑minute journey.

Which airports serve Mumbai and Dubai, and how far are they from the city centre?

Dubai is served by Dubai International Airport (DXB), which has three terminals and lies about 15 km south of Dubai’s city centre, reachable by metro, taxi or bus. Mumbai is served by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM), which has two terminals and is situated roughly 8 km north of Mumbai’s central business district, with easy access via the Mumbai Airport Express Line, taxis and ride‑share services.

How many flights operate between Dubai and Mumbai, and what is the distance?

On an average day, around 12‑15 flights operate between Dubai International Airport (DXB) and Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM), covering a great‑circle distance of approximately 1,926 kilometres. This frequency includes multiple daily services by Emirates, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet and Air Arabia, providing ample options for both business and leisure travellers throughout the week.

What is the best time of year to visit Mumbai, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Mumbai is between November and February, when the city experiences mild winter weather with temperatures ranging from 20 °C to 30 °C and low humidity. This period follows the monsoon season, offering clear skies and comfortable evenings for sightseeing. The summer months (March‑May) are hot and humid, while June‑September bring heavy rains, making travel less pleasant.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo is generally the cheapest airline on the Dubai‑Mumbai route, offering basic economy fares that omit complimentary meals and checked baggage. The trade‑off is reduced in‑flight service and fewer premium amenities compared with full‑service carriers such as Emirates or Air India, which include meals, baggage allowance and lounge access but charge higher ticket prices.

What ground transport options are available from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport to Mumbai city centre?

From Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) to Mumbai city centre you can take the Mumbai Airport Express Line (suburban train) to Andheri or Vile Parle, a city bus, prepaid taxis, or ride‑share services. The express line reaches the city in about 15 minutes, while taxis and ride‑share journeys typically take 20‑30 minutes depending on traffic, providing convenient choices for all budgets.
