Travelling from London to Mumbai: what you need to know

  • Depart from London Heathrow Airport (LHR), the main hub for long‑haul services; other London airports such as Gatwick (LGW) have occasional connections but no regular non‑stop flights to Mumbai.
  • Arrive at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM), the sole international gateway for the city.
  • Average non‑stop flight time is about 9 hours 30 minutes, covering roughly 7 213 km.
  • Mumbai is 5.5 hours ahead of London (UTC+5:30 versus UTC+0, or UTC+1 during British Summer Time).
  • The LHR‑BOM pairing is the busiest UK‑India trunk, with multiple daily services.
  • Arrive at least two hours before departure for international flights; allow extra time during peak holiday periods.

How to find the cheapest flights from London to Mumbai

  • Book 8‑12 weeks ahead for the best balance of price and seat availability.
  • January and February are historically the cheapest months, with lower demand after the New Year rush.
  • Set up a price‑alert on a flight‑comparison site to be notified of drops.
  • Use the “price‑lowest” or “duration‑shortest” filters and experiment with flexible dates, especially mid‑week departures.

Flying from London: Heathrow Airport

  • Non‑stop carriers from LHR to BOM include British Airways, Air India and Virgin Atlantic.
  • Common one‑stop hubs are Dubai (DXB), Doha (DOH), Istanbul (IST) and Frankfurt (FRA).
  • Heathrow lies about 24 km west of central London; the Heathrow Express reaches Paddington in 15 minutes, then the Underground or taxis take another 20‑30 minutes.
  • By car, the journey is 45‑60 minutes depending on traffic; there are several long‑stay car parks and ride‑share options.

Arriving in Mumbai: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport

  • BOM is roughly 8 km north‑north‑west of Mumbai’s business district; travel time by car is 30‑45 minutes, subject to peak‑hour congestion.
  • The Airport Express Line of the Mumbai Suburban Railway connects the terminal to the city centre (CST) in about 45 minutes.
  • App‑based cabs, prepaid taxis and auto‑rickshaws are widely available; a typical city‑centre taxi fare is INR 300‑500.
  • Once in the city, the local train network, metro (Line 1) and extensive bus system provide affordable onward travel.

Best time to go to Mumbai

  • November is the most popular month, coinciding with festivals such as Diwali and pleasant winter weather.
  • May and early June are quieter and often cheaper, though temperatures rise sharply.
  • Summer (March‑May) sees highs of 30‑35 °C with high humidity; the monsoon (June‑September) brings 27‑32 °C and frequent showers; winter (November‑February) offers 20‑30 °C, ideal for sightseeing.

More about Mumbai

  • Iconic sights include the Gateway of India, Marine Drive’s promenade and the historic Elephanta Caves accessed by ferry.
  • Explore the vibrant neighbourhoods of Bandra (street art, cafés) and Colaba (markets, colonial architecture).
  • The city’s culinary scene ranges from street‑side vada pav to upscale seafood at the Taj Mahal Palace.
  • For culture, visit the Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya museum and the bustling Crawford Market.

Explore more of India

  • Delhi, about 1 400 km north of Mumbai, boasts the Red Fort, Qutub Minar and a thriving food scene.
  • Jaipur, roughly 1 100 km north‑west, is famed for Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal and pink‑hued streets.
  • Goa, around 950 km west of Mumbai, offers palm‑lined beaches, Portuguese architecture and a relaxed nightlife.

Finding cheap flights from London to Mumbai: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from London to Mumbai?

To find cheap flights from London to Mumbai, search flexibly across dates, use nearby airports, and set price alerts on this page. Check fares from both Heathrow (LHR) and Gatwick (LGW) as low‑cost carriers sometimes operate from the latter. Browse a three‑month window and sort by cheapest, then filter for flights with one stop in the Middle East, which are often cheaper than direct services. Book 8‑10 weeks ahead and monitor price drops before confirming.

What is the cheapest way to fly from London to Mumbai?

The cheapest way to fly from London to Mumbai is to combine a low‑cost carrier on the outbound leg with a major airline on the return, or to use a single full‑service airline offering economy promotional fares, typically booked several months in advance and flown from a secondary London airport such as Gatwick (LGW) if available. Booking a round‑trip and selecting flexible dates often reduces the per‑ticket cost compared with two one‑way tickets.

When is the best time to book a flight from London to Mumbai to get the lowest price?

The best time to book a flight from London to Mumbai for the lowest price is around eight to twelve weeks before departure, with a slight advantage to booking on Tuesday or Wednesday evenings when airlines release fare updates. Early‑morning searches often show lower fares because inventory is refreshed overnight, and setting a price alert on this page will notify you of any drops during the optimal booking window.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from London to Mumbai?

The cheapest month to fly from London to Mumbai is usually February, March, or early November, when demand is low after the winter holiday peak and before the monsoon season begins. These periods see average economy fares 15‑20 % below the summer high, and airlines often run promotional sales in January for travel in the following months, making it an ideal time to secure a low‑cost ticket.

How long is the flight from London to Mumbai?

The non‑stop flight from London Heathrow (LHR) to Mumbai (BOM) takes approximately nine hours and fifteen minutes, covering 7,213 km and crossing three time zones, so arrival is about four hours ahead of London time. Airlines usually schedule the outbound service in the early evening London time, arriving in Mumbai early the next morning. If you choose a one‑stop itinerary via the Middle East, total travel time expands to twelve‑sixteen hours, depending on layover length and airline, and may involve an overnight stay at the hub.

Which airlines fly from London to Mumbai?

Airlines that operate between London Heathrow (LHR) and Mumbai (BOM) include British Airways and Virgin Atlantic, which run daily non‑stop services, as well as Air India and Vistara, which offer a mix of direct and one‑stop flights. Emirates provides a popular one‑stop option via Dubai (DXB) and Qatar Airways via Doha (DOH), both with multiple daily departures. All carriers sell economy, premium economy and business class seats, and most participate in frequent‑flyer programmes that allow mileage accrual on this long‑haul route.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from London to Mumbai?

There are direct, non‑stop flights from London Heathrow (LHR) to Mumbai (BOM) operated by British Airways and Air India, each typically offering one daily departure in both directions. British Airways usually departs in the early evening London time and arrives in Mumbai early the next morning, while Air India often flies in the late afternoon, arriving late at night. Both carriers use wide‑body aircraft such as the Boeing 777 or Airbus A380, providing economy, premium economy and business cabins on a straight‑through journey without a change of plane.

How much does a flight from London to Mumbai usually cost and what drives the price?

A typical economy fare from London to Mumbai ranges from £350 to £750 round‑trip, depending on factors such as travel season, how far in advance you book, and whether you choose a direct service. Prices rise sharply during the European summer, Indian holidays like Diwali and school break periods, while the lowest fares appear in February, March and early November when demand is soft. Adding a stopover in the Middle East can reduce the fare, but may increase total travel time and ancillary costs such as airport transfers.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

The cheapest day to fly from London to Mumbai is usually Tuesday or Wednesday, and departing in the early morning, around 06:00‑08:00 GMT, often yields lower fares because demand is weaker than on weekend evenings. Mid‑week flights benefit from reduced business‑travel pressure and airlines frequently release discounted seats after the Monday booking surge. Checking flights that leave before 09:00 GMT and returning on a Wednesday or Thursday can further cut the total cost, especially when combined with flexible date searches on this page.

Which airports serve Mumbai (and London), and how far are they from the city centre?

London is served by Heathrow Airport (LHR), which has four terminals and lies approximately 24 km west of Central London, reachable by the Heathrow Express train in 15 minutes or by the Piccadilly Line of the London Underground. Mumbai is served by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) with two terminals, situated about 8 km north of the city centre, and is connected by the Mumbai Airport Express train, local buses and prepaid taxi services that reach downtown in roughly 20‑30 minutes depending on traffic.

How many flights operate between London and Mumbai, and what is the distance?

Around ten to twelve scheduled flights operate each week between London Heathrow (LHR) and Mumbai (BOM), comprising both daily non‑stop services by British Airways and Air India and several one‑stop options via Dubai or Doha. The great‑circle distance between the two airports is roughly 7,213 km, which translates to a typical flight time of nine to ten hours non‑stop. During the Indian festival season and European summer, airlines often add extra frequencies, raising the weekly total to as many as fifteen departures.

What is the best time of year to visit Mumbai, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Mumbai is between November and February, when the city experiences cool, dry weather with daytime temperatures between 20 °C and 30 °C and low humidity, ideal for sightseeing and outdoor markets. This period also coincides with major events such as the Mumbai Marathon and the International Film Festival. The monsoon season runs from June to September, bringing heavy showers and high humidity, while March to May can be hot and humid, often exceeding 35 °C.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

Vistara frequently provides the lowest base fare on the London‑Mumbai route, especially when booked with flexible dates and a stopover in Delhi, but the trade‑off is a limited number of weekly departures and a smaller premium‑cabin offering compared with British Airways or Air India. Those legacy carriers operate daily non‑stop services, have a wider range of business‑class seats and allow passengers to earn and redeem Avios or Flying Returns miles. Choosing Vistara can save money, but may require more planning around flight times and connections.

Do UK citizens need a visa to travel to Mumbai, and what are the entry requirements?

UK citizens need an electronic travel authorization (e‑Visa) to enter India, which can be applied for online and is valid for tourism, business or medical visits for up to 60 days; the e‑Visa must be approved before departure and printed for immigration. Travelers must hold a passport with at least six months validity, a return or onward ticket, and may be asked to show proof of sufficient funds and accommodation. No visa‑on‑arrival is available at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport.
