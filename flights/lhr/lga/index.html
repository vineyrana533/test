Travelling from London to New York: what you need to know

  • Depart from Heathrow Airport (LHR), London’s main international hub with four terminals.
  • Arrive at LaGuardia Airport (LGA), the closest New York airport to Manhattan, with a single terminal.
  • Average non‑stop flight time is about 7 hours 45 minutes.
  • London is UTC+0 (or UTC+1 in summer) and New York is UTC‑5 (or UTC‑4 in summer), giving a 5‑hour time‑difference for most of the year.
  • The LHR‑LGA pairing is the busiest Heathrow‑to‑New‑York corridor.
  • Aim to be at the departure hall at least two hours before an international flight, and a little earlier during peak summer and holiday periods.

How to find the cheapest flights from London to New York

  • Booking 8‑12 weeks ahead usually yields the best fares; last‑minute deals are rare on this transatlantic lane.
  • January is traditionally the cheapest month, with lower demand after the holiday season.
  • Set up a price‑alert on a comparison site; you’ll be notified when fares drop below your target.
  • Use the “cheapest” or “fastest” sort filters and then refine by airline or departure time to balance price against convenience.

Flying from London: Heathrow Airport

  • Non‑stop carriers from Heathrow to New York include British Airways and Virgin Atlantic.
  • Common one‑stop hubs are Dublin (DUB), Reykjavik (KEF), Amsterdam (AMS) and Toronto (YYZ).
  • Heathrow sits roughly 23 km west of Central London.
  • By car or taxi the journey takes about 45‑60 minutes, depending on traffic.
  • The Heathrow Express reaches Paddington in 15 minutes; a further 30‑minute tube ride (Piccadilly line) brings you to the terminal, making public transport a reliable option.

Arriving in New York: LaGuardia Airport

  • LaGuardia is about 13 km (8 miles) east of Midtown Manhattan.
  • A car or rideshare typically needs 20‑30 minutes, but rush‑hour traffic can push the time beyond an hour.
  • The M60 SBS bus links the terminal with the Astoria subway station; from there the N/W trains reach Manhattan in roughly 30‑45 minutes.
  • Taxis, rideshares and the city’s extensive bus network provide additional ways to reach the city centre once you land.

Best time to go to New York

  • June sees the highest visitor numbers, thanks to warm weather and many festivals.
  • January offers lower prices and thinner crowds, making it the most economical month.
  • Summer temperatures average 25‑30 °C, while winter averages 0‑5 °C, with occasional sub‑zero spells.
  • Spring (April‑May) and early autumn (September‑October) provide mild weather and a good balance between price and comfort.

More about New York

  • Iconic sights include Times Square, Central Park, the Statue of Liberty and the Brooklyn Bridge.
  • World‑class museums such as the Metropolitan Museum of Art and MoMA draw art lovers.
  • Vibrant neighbourhoods like Williamsburg (Brooklyn), Harlem (Manhattan) and Greenwich Village offer distinct cultural experiences.
  • The city’s extensive subway system makes it easy to hop between attractions without a car.

Explore more of the United States

  • Boston, Massachusetts – about 340 km northeast of New York; historic Freedom Trail, Harvard University and the Boston Common.
  • Washington, D.C. – roughly 360 km southwest; the National Mall, Smithsonian museums and the Capitol.
  • Philadelphia, Pennsylvania – around 150 km southwest; Independence Hall, the Liberty Bell and the vibrant Reading Terminal Market.
  • Each city is reachable by a 1‑2‑hour train ride (Amtrak) or a short domestic flight, expanding your North‑East itinerary.

Finding cheap flights from London to New York: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from London to New York?

You can find cheap flights from London Heathrow (LHR) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) by keeping your travel dates flexible, searching across the whole month, and targeting the off‑peak months of January, February and early November. Booking 6‑8 weeks in advance usually yields the best fares, and checking nearby airports such as Gatwick (LGW) or Stansted (STN) for departures and Newark (EWR) for arrivals can uncover lower‑priced options. Set price alerts on this page to be notified when a fare drops.

What is the cheapest way to fly from London to New York?

The cheapest way to fly from London to New York is often to combine a low‑cost carrier to a US hub such as Newark (EWR) or New York JFK and then take a short domestic flight or ground transport to LaGuardia (LGA). Airlines like Icelandair via Reykjavik or Aer Lingus via Dublin regularly offer lower‑priced connecting itineraries, while direct services on British Airways, American Airlines or Delta tend to be pricier but faster.

When is the best time to book a flight from London to New York to get the lowest price?

The best time to book a flight from London Heathrow to New York LaGuardia for the lowest price is typically 6‑8 weeks before departure, with an additional dip in fares when you book in early January for travel later in the year. Monitoring fares throughout the week and securing tickets on a Tuesday or Wednesday often results in a lower price, especially if you avoid the high‑demand summer travel window.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from London to New York?

The cheapest month to fly from London to New York is usually February, followed closely by January and early November, when demand is low and airlines run promotional fares. Winter travel benefits from reduced tourism and business traffic, so average economy round‑trip prices drop by 15‑25 % compared with the peak summer months of June to August.

How long is the flight from London to New York?

A non‑stop flight from London Heathrow (LHR) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) typically lasts about 7 hours and 30 minutes, covering a great‑circle distance of roughly 5,537 kilometres. Flight times can vary by a few minutes depending on wind direction, and connecting itineraries may add several hours to the total travel time.

Which airlines fly from London to New York?

British Airways, American Airlines and Delta Air Lines operate direct services between London Heathrow and New York LaGuardia. Additional carriers such as Icelandair, Aer Lingus and United Airlines provide one‑stop options via Reykjavik, Dublin or Newark, while low‑cost airlines like LEVEL and Norwegian (when operating) can be found on connecting routes that end at LaGuardia after a short domestic hop.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from London to New York?

Yes, there are direct non‑stop flights between London Heathrow (LHR) and New York LaGuardia (LGA) operated by British Airways, American Airlines and Delta Air Lines. These services run multiple times per day, especially during peak travel periods, and typically take around 7½ hours each way.

How much does a flight from London to New York usually cost and what drives the price?

Economy round‑trip fares between London Heathrow and New York LaGuardia generally range from £300 to £800, with lower ends appearing in winter and higher ends in summer. Prices are driven by demand cycles, fuel costs, booking window, day of the week, and whether the flight is direct or includes a stop. Additional fees for baggage, seat selection and meals can increase the total cost, especially on low‑cost carriers.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Flying on Tuesdays or Wednesdays and choosing early‑morning departures (before 09:00) or late‑night flights (after 22:00) from London to New York usually yields lower fares. Mid‑week flights avoid the business‑travel surge that inflates prices on Mondays and Fridays, while off‑peak departure times reduce demand and therefore cost.

Which airports serve New York and London, and how far are they from the city centre?

London Heathrow Airport (LHR) lies about 14 km west of central London and has four terminals. New York LaGuardia Airport (LGA) is roughly 13 km east of Manhattan, with a single terminal serving domestic and short‑haul international flights. Additional airports include London Gatwick (LGW) 45 km south, and New York John F. Kennedy (JFK) 26 km from Manhattan and Newark Liberty (EWR) 24 km from the city centre.

How many flights operate between London and New York, and what is the distance?

Around 10‑12 non‑stop flights per day operate between London Heathrow and New York LaGuardia, provided by British Airways, American Airlines and Delta Air Lines. The great‑circle distance between the two airports is approximately 5,537 kilometres, making it one of the busiest trans‑Atlantic corridors in the world.

What is the best time of year to visit New York, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit New York is in spring (April‑June) or autumn (September‑October) when temperatures are mild, ranging from 10 °C to 22 °C, and humidity is low. Summer brings hot, humid days often above 30 °C, while winter can be bitterly cold with temperatures below 0 °C and occasional snowfall, which may affect travel plans.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

The cheapest airlines on the London‑New York corridor are typically low‑cost carriers such as Icelandair or Aer Lingus that operate one‑stop itineraries via Reykjavik or Dublin. The trade‑off is a longer total travel time, usually 10‑12 hours including layovers, and additional fees for checked baggage and seat selection, whereas direct carriers like British Airways provide a faster 7½‑hour flight but at a higher base fare.

Do I need a visa to travel from London to New York?

UK citizens travelling to New York do not need a traditional visa for tourism or business trips of up to 90 days; they must obtain an approved Electronic System for Travel Authorization (ESTA) under the U.S. Visa Waiver Programme before departure. The ESTA application is completed online, costs a small fee, and is usually valid for two years or until the passport expires, whichever comes first.
