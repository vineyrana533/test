Travelling from New York to Dallas: what you need to know

  • Depart from LaGuardia Airport (LGA) in Queens; arrive at Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) in the north‑west Dallas metro area.
  • The nonstop sector covers roughly 2 231 km and typically lasts 3 h 45 m in the air, plus climb‑out and approach time.
  • New York operates on Eastern Time (UTC‑5 in winter, UTC‑4 in summer) while Dallas is on Central Time (UTC‑6/UTC‑5); the clock is one hour behind in Dallas.
  • LGA‑DFW is the busiest domestic pairing between the two cities, with several daily services.
  • For a domestic flight, aim to be at the gate at least two hours before departure; add extra time in summer when security lines lengthen.

How to find the cheapest flights from New York to Dallas

  • Book 6‑8 weeks ahead for the best balance of price and seat choice; last‑minute fares can rise sharply.
  • Historical data shows January and early February produce the lowest average fare on this corridor.
  • Set a price‑alert on a comparison site; the alert will notify you when a fare drops below your target.
  • When using the search tools, sort by “cheapest” first, then refine by “shortest duration” to spot a good value nonstop.

Flying from New York: LaGuardia Airport

  • Non‑stop carriers from LGA include American Airlines, Delta Air Lines and Southwest Airlines; each runs multiple daily flights.
  • Common one‑stop hubs are Chicago O’Hare (ORD), Denver International (DEN) and Atlanta Hartsfield‑Jackson (ATL), used by United and other legacy carriers.
  • LGA sits about 13 km (8 mi) from Manhattan; a taxi or rideshare takes 20‑30 min in light traffic.
  • Public‑transport options combine the Q70 SBS bus to the 7‑train, then the 7 to the 53rd St‑Hancock, totaling roughly 45 min to the terminals.

Arriving in Dallas: Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport

  • DFW is roughly 32 km (20 mi) north‑west of downtown Dallas; the drive usually takes 30‑35 min without rush‑hour traffic.
  • The DART Orange Line rail links Terminal A to downtown Dallas in about 45 min, with a free shuttle to the terminal.
  • Taxis, rideshares and airport shuttles are widely available; a typical fare to the city centre is US $35‑$45.
  • Once in Dallas, the extensive highway network (I‑35E, I‑30) and DART light‑rail make it easy to reach attractions and suburbs.

Best time to go to Dallas

  • May is the most popular month, coinciding with the Dallas Blooms garden show and warm but not extreme weather.
  • January offers the cheapest and quietest travel period; hotel rates and flight demand dip after the holiday season.
  • Summer temperatures regularly sit between 35 °C and 38 °C, with high humidity; winter lows range from 5 °C to 12 °C, occasionally dropping near freezing.

More about Dallas

  • The Sixth Floor Museum at Dealey Plaza tells the story of President Kennedy’s assassination.
  • The Dallas Arts District, the nation’s largest urban arts district, houses the Dallas Museum of Art and the Nasher Sculpture Center.
  • The Dallas Arboretum and Botanical Garden on the shores of White Rock Lake provides seasonal displays and festivals.
  • Deep Ellum’s historic brick streets host live‑music venues, street art, and a vibrant nightlife scene.

Explore more of Texas

  • Austin lies about 320 km (200 mi) south of Dallas; the capital is famed for the Texas State Capitol, live‑music Sixth Street, and the annual South‑by‑Southwest festival.
  • San Antonio is roughly 440 km (275 mi) south‑west; its River Walk, the Alamo, and the historic Missions are major draws.
  • Houston sits about 380 km (235 mi) east; the Space Center Houston, Museum District and vibrant culinary scene attract visitors year‑round.
