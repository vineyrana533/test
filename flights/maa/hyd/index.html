Overview of Flying Between Chennai and Hyderabad

The flight corridor between Chennai International Airport (MAA) and Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) is one of the busiest domestic routes in India. Covering 508 kilometres, the journey typically takes just over an hour and a quarter on a direct flight. Major carriers such as IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Vistara and Go First operate frequent services, with a mix of early‑morning and late‑afternoon departures to suit business and leisure travellers.

Because the distance is short, the route is highly competitive, and airlines often adjust fares in response to seasonal demand, making it worthwhile to monitor pricing closely.

Chennai International Airport – Getting There and From

Chennai International Airport sits 12 kilometres west of the city centre on the coast. It has three terminals: Terminal 1 for domestic flights, Terminal 2 for international departures, and Terminal 3 for domestic arrivals. The airport is well connected to the city by the Airport Express bus, the 12‑hour Metro rail extension, and a dedicated taxi stand. For those arriving by road, the NH4 and the Chennai‑Bangalore Expressway provide quick access.

The airport’s parking facilities are extensive, and a dedicated car‑hire desk offers a range of vehicles for short‑term and long‑term needs.

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport – Getting There and From

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport lies 12 kilometres east of Hyderabad’s city centre, on the outskirts of Secunderabad. It is a single‑terminal airport, but it is divided into two concourses for domestic and international flights. Ground transport options include the Hyderabad Metro’s Airport Line, city buses, and a sizeable taxi fleet. The airport also offers a dedicated car‑hire service.

Passengers can reach the city centre in 20–30 minutes by taxi or metro, depending on traffic conditions.

Finding the Cheapest Fares on the Chennai‑Hyderabad Route

Because the route is highly competitive, fares fluctuate quickly. The best strategy is to use fare‑comparison tools that allow you to set alerts for price drops. Booking 3–6 weeks in advance often yields the lowest rates, especially if you avoid peak travel days. Flexible travel dates can also unlock significant savings, as fares tend to be lower on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

Check the airlines’ own websites for special promotions, as they sometimes offer exclusive discounts that are not reflected on third‑party sites.

Best Time of Year to Travel and What to Expect

The Chennai‑Hyderabad corridor is open year‑round, but the most pleasant weather is from October to March when temperatures are moderate and humidity is lower. During the summer months (April–June) temperatures can exceed 40°C, which may make early‑morning flights preferable. The monsoon season (July–September) brings heavy rain but also lower fares and fewer crowds.

If you prefer a lively atmosphere, plan your trip around major festivals such as Sankranti in January or the Hyderabad Festival of Lights in December.

Getting Around Hyderabad – Highlights and Transport

Hyderabad’s city centre is served by the Hyderabad Metro, which connects key districts and the airport. The city’s old fort area, Charminar, and the modern HITEC City are easily reachable by auto‑rickshaws or app‑based cabs. For a deeper cultural experience, a short trip to the historic Golconda Fort or the serene Hussain Sagar Lake offers a pleasant contrast to the bustling city.

Public transport is reliable, but for a more comfortable journey, consider renting a car or using a private transfer for longer trips outside the city.

Finding cheap flights from Chennai to Hyderabad: frequently asked questions

How long is the flight from Chennai to Hyderabad?

A direct flight from Chennai to Hyderabad takes about 1 hour 15 minutes. The 508‑km journey is served by multiple carriers, so you can expect a similar duration for most non‑stop services. Flight times are typically between 1 hour 10 minutes and 1 hour 30 minutes, depending on wind conditions and departure time. The short flight makes it convenient for business or leisure travel without the need for overnight stays.

Which airlines fly from Chennai to Hyderabad?

The main airlines operating between Chennai and Hyderabad are IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Vistara, Go First and Alliance Air. IndiGo is the most frequent, offering the greatest number of daily flights. Air India provides a full‑service option, while Vistara offers premium economy and business seats. Low‑cost carriers such as SpiceJet and Go First cater to budget travellers, and Alliance Air serves regional routes with smaller aircraft.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Chennai to Hyderabad?

Yes, all major carriers offer non‑stop services on this route. IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Vistara and Go First operate direct flights from Chennai International Airport (MAA) to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD). The short distance means that every flight on the schedule is a quick, non‑stop hop, making it easy to plan a same‑day return if needed.

How much does a flight from Chennai to Hyderabad cost / what drives the price?

Fares typically range from ₹1,500 to ₹4,500 for a one‑way economy ticket, depending on airline, booking window, season and seat class. Low‑cost carriers such as IndiGo and SpiceJet usually offer the lowest prices, while Air India and Vistara command a premium for extra legroom and in‑flight services. Prices rise during peak holiday periods and around major festivals, and can drop sharply when booked well in advance.

What is the cheapest month/time to fly from Chennai to Hyderabad?

January and February are the cheapest months to fly, as the low season coincides with the post‑festival lull and mild weather. During these months, airlines often run promotional fares and seat inventory is higher, driving prices down. Mid‑week departures in early mornings or late afternoons tend to be cheaper than weekend flights, which are usually priced at a premium.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Mid‑week flights, especially on Tuesdays or Wednesdays, and departures around 14:00–16:00 local time, usually fetch the lowest fares. Airlines schedule fewer passengers on these days, so they offer discounts to fill seats. Early‑morning or late‑evening flights can also be cheaper, but the convenience of a midday departure often balances cost and comfort.

How far in advance should I book flights from Chennai to Hyderabad?

Booking 6–8 weeks in advance generally yields the best prices, though last‑minute deals can appear during off‑peak periods. For peak festivals or holiday seasons, it is advisable to book 3–4 months ahead to secure a seat at a reasonable rate. Monitoring fare alerts and setting price thresholds can help you capture price drops as the departure date approaches.

Which airports serve Hyderabad (and Chennai), and how far are they from the city centre?

Chennai International Airport (MAA) sits about 15 km south of Chennai’s city centre, accessible by the Airport Express Metro, taxis and city buses. Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) lies roughly 30 km east of Hyderabad’s city core, with a dedicated Airport Express Metro line, app‑based rides and a city bus service linking the airport to key districts.

How many flights operate between Chennai and Hyderabad?

There are about ten to twelve daily flights, with departures spread from early morning to late evening. IndiGo alone offers up to eight flights a day, while Air India, SpiceJet, Vistara and Go First add several more. The high frequency ensures that travellers have multiple options for timing and price, making it easy to find a convenient slot.

What is the flight distance between Chennai and Hyderabad?

The great‑circle distance between MAA and HYD is approximately 508 km (315 miles). This short hop is one of the most efficient domestic routes in India, with a flight time of just over an hour. The distance makes the route popular for business commuters and weekend get‑aways, as it requires minimal travel time and jet lag is negligible.

What is the best time of year to visit Hyderabad, and what is the weather like?

The period from October to March offers pleasant temperatures between 20–30 °C and low humidity, making it ideal for sightseeing and cultural festivals. The monsoon season from June to September brings heavy rainfall and higher temperatures, which can dampen outdoor plans. If you prefer cooler weather and fewer crowds, plan your visit during the winter months, especially early December.

How do I get the cheapest flight from Chennai to Hyderabad?

To secure the cheapest fare, set up price alerts on comparison sites, book mid‑week, avoid peak holiday periods, and opt for low‑cost carriers such as IndiGo or SpiceJet. Use flexible dates to compare prices, and consider booking a return ticket if you need to travel back, as many airlines offer a discount for round‑trip bookings. Checking the airline’s own website can sometimes reveal exclusive promotions.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo and SpiceJet are typically the cheapest options on the Chennai‑Hyderabad route. The trade‑off is a more utilitarian cabin experience: fewer complimentary meals, limited legroom, and a pay‑as‑you‑go baggage policy. For budget travellers, the savings outweigh the reduced amenities, but those seeking extra comfort may prefer Air India or Vistara, which offer premium economy and business seats.

How can I reach Rajiv Gandhi International Airport from Hyderabad city centre?

From Hyderabad city centre, the Airport Express Metro line (Blue Line) runs directly to the airport, taking about 30 minutes from the city hub. Alternatively, you can take a taxi, app‑based ride or the city bus service, all of which depart regularly and cover the 30‑km distance in roughly 45–60 minutes depending on traffic. The metro is the most cost‑effective and reliable option during peak hours.
