Travelling from Chennai to Hyderabad: what you need to know

  • Depart from Chennai International Airport (MAA) and arrive at Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD).
  • The average direct flight covers 508 km and takes roughly 1 hour 10 minutes.
  • Both cities are in the same time zone (Indian Standard Time), so there is no time‑difference to adjust for.
  • The most frequent pairing is MAA → HYD; most airlines operate daily services on this corridor.
  • For international flights, it is advisable to arrive at the departure terminal about 2 hours before the scheduled departure, especially during the peak monsoon season when security checks can take longer.

How to find the cheapest flights from Chennai to Hyderabad

  • The best window to book is 4–6 weeks ahead of travel; fares tend to rise as the date approaches.
  • The cheapest months to fly are October and November, when post‑festival demand is lower.
  • Set up a price alert on a fare‑comparison site; it will notify you when a drop occurs.
  • Use the filter to sort by ‘cheapest’ or ‘shortest flight’ to see the most economical and time‑efficient options.

Flying from Chennai: Chennai International Airport

  • IndiGo, Air India, Vistara, SpiceJet and GoAir all operate non‑stop services from MAA to HYD.
  • Common one‑stop hubs for alternative routes are Delhi (DEL) and Bangalore (BLR).
  • The airport sits about 30 km north of the city centre; a car journey takes roughly 45 minutes under normal traffic.
  • The Airport Express bus runs every 10–15 minutes and covers the distance in 30–35 minutes.

Arriving in Hyderabad: Rajiv Gandhi International Airport

  • HYD is located roughly 30 km south of Hyderabad’s city centre.
  • By car the journey takes about 45 minutes; the Airport Metro line offers a 30‑minute ride into the city.
  • Local buses, taxis and app‑based cabs (Uber, Ola) provide convenient onward transport from the airport.
  • The metro’s Red Line connects the airport with key districts such as Secunderabad, Banjara Hills and the historic Charminar area.

Best time to go to Hyderabad

  • November is the peak tourist month, with pleasant evenings after the summer heat.
  • February offers lower prices and still comfortable temperatures, making it a quieter alternative.
  • Summer (June‑August) temperatures range from 30 °C to 40 °C, while winter (December‑February) averages between 15 °C and 25 °C.
  • The monsoon season (June‑September) brings heavy rainfall but also lush green surroundings.

More about Hyderabad

  • Charminar, the iconic 16‑th‑century monument, is a must‑visit in the Old City.
  • Golconda Fort offers panoramic views and a fascinating history of the Qutb Shahi dynasty.
  • Hussain Sagar Lake and the nearby Necklace Road provide scenic walks and boat rides.
  • The Salar Jung Museum houses a world‑class collection of art and antiques.

Explore more of India

  • Bangalore (BLR) is about 350 km south of Hyderabad; a 4‑hour drive or a 1‑hour flight.
  • Delhi (DEL) lies roughly 1,400 km north; a 2‑hour flight or a 20‑hour train journey.
  • Mumbai (BOM) is around 1,200 km west; a 2‑hour flight or a 24‑hour train ride.
  • Each city offers distinct cultural experiences: tech hub and gardens in Bangalore, historic monuments in Delhi, and the bustling Marine Drive in Mumbai.

Finding cheap flights from Chennai to Hyderabad: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Chennai to Hyderabad?

Start by setting flexible travel dates and searching for mid‑week departures, as fares are usually lower on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Avoid peak festival periods such as Diwali and the Indian New Year, and book 1–3 months in advance. Compare fares on this page, and consider nearby airports like Tirupati or Bangalore for alternative routes that may offer cheaper tickets. Use price alerts to monitor fluctuations, and book when the price drops to the lowest range you see.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Chennai to Hyderabad?

The cheapest way to fly from Chennai to Hyderabad is to book an economy ticket with a low‑cost carrier such as IndiGo or SpiceJet. Choose a mid‑week departure, avoid peak holiday periods, and book at least a month in advance. These airlines offer the lowest base fares, and you can often find discounted promotions if you monitor the offers on this page.

When is the best time to book a flight from Chennai to Hyderabad to get the lowest price?

Domestic flights from Chennai to Hyderabad are usually cheapest when booked 30 to 90 days before the departure date. Avoid last‑minute bookings, which can drive prices up, particularly during school holidays or festival periods. Booking early also gives you a better chance to secure a seat on a low‑cost carrier and take advantage of any promotional fares that may appear.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Chennai to Hyderabad?

The cheapest months to fly from Chennai to Hyderabad are January, February, and March, when demand is lower and many airlines run promotional fares. Early October, just after the monsoon season, also offers competitive prices. Avoid peak festival months such as December (Christmas and New Year) and April (Bengaluru festival season), when fares typically rise.

How long is the flight from Chennai to Hyderabad?

The flight from Chennai International Airport (MAA) to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) covers a distance of 508 km and typically takes between 1 hour 15 minutes and 1 hour 30 minutes, depending on wind conditions and the specific airline.

Which airlines fly from Chennai to Hyderabad?

Daily non‑stop services between Chennai and Hyderabad are operated by several carriers, including IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Vistara, and Go First. These airlines provide multiple flights each day, offering a range of seat classes and fare options for passengers.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Chennai to Hyderabad?

Yes, all major airlines that serve the Chennai–Hyderabad corridor operate direct, non‑stop flights between Chennai International Airport (MAA) and Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD). This means you can travel the 508 km distance in a single flight without any layovers.

How much does a flight from Chennai to Hyderabad usually cost / what drives the price?

Economy fares for a one‑way ticket from Chennai to Hyderabad usually range from about ₹2,000 to ₹6,000. Prices are driven by factors such as the time of year, how far in advance you book, the airline chosen, and the seat class. Low‑cost carriers like IndiGo and SpiceJet tend to offer the lowest base fares, while full‑service airlines may charge a premium for added amenities.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

The best day of the week to fly for a lower fare is typically Tuesday or Wednesday, when airlines often release discounted seats. Early‑morning departures between 6 am and 8 am also tend to be cheaper than later flights, as demand is lower during those hours. Booking a flight on these days and times can help you secure a better price.

Which airports serve Hyderabad (and Chennai), and how far are they from the city centre?

Chennai International Airport (MAA) is located about 15 km north of Chennai’s city centre, while Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) lies roughly 30 km south of Hyderabad’s city centre. Both airports are well connected to their respective city centres by metro, bus, and taxi services, making them convenient hubs for travellers.

How many flights operate between Chennai and Hyderabad, and what is the distance?

Between Chennai and Hyderabad, airlines operate approximately 20 to 25 daily flights, covering the 508 km distance. Most carriers, such as IndiGo, Air India, and SpiceJet, offer at least two non‑stop services per day, providing passengers with flexible departure times throughout the day.

What is the best time of year to visit Hyderabad, and what is the weather like?

The best time of year to visit Hyderabad is from October to March, when temperatures are mild, ranging from 15 °C to 30 °C, and the humidity is lower. April to June can be hot, with temperatures exceeding 35 °C, while July to September is the monsoon season, bringing frequent rain and higher humidity.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo and SpiceJet typically provide the cheapest fares on the Chennai–Hyderabad route. The trade‑off is that these low‑cost carriers offer smaller cabin space, limited complimentary meals, and lower baggage allowances compared to full‑service airlines such as Air India or Vistara, which provide complimentary meals, larger seat pitch, and higher baggage limits.

What is the best way to travel from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport to Hyderabad city centre?

The most convenient way to travel from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport to Hyderabad city centre is by the Airport Express bus or a taxi using the Hyderabad Airport Metro link. Both options take approximately 45 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic, and cost between ₹200 and ₹400, offering a balance of speed, comfort, and affordability.
