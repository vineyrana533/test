Overview of flying Mumbai to Dubai

The route between Mumbai and Dubai covers roughly 1,926 kilometres and is served by a number of airlines each operating daily flights. The journey is typically around three hours and forty‑five minutes when flown directly, although connecting flights can extend the total travel time to five or six hours.

Mumbai operates from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport, which lies about 27 kilometres north of the city centre. Dubai is served mainly by Dubai International Airport, situated just 4 kilometres from downtown. The time zone difference is 1.5 hours – Dubai is one and a half hours behind Mumbai – which can affect arrival times and jet lag considerations.

The route is popular for business and leisure travel, and the frequent service means travellers can choose from a range of departure times throughout the day.

Getting to and from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport

The airport is divided into two terminals. Terminal 1 mainly handles domestic flights, while Terminal 2 is used for international services, including the Mumbai‑Dubai corridor.

The airport is well connected to the city by the Mumbai Metro Line 1, which stops at the airport and offers a quick 30‑minute journey to the city centre. The local train network (Western Line) also serves the airport, with a 45‑minute ride to the main railway stations.

Taxis, app‑based cabs and company‑run shuttles are widely available outside the arrivals area. The average fare to the city centre is around ₹1,200 to ₹1,500, depending on traffic and time of day.

Getting to and from Dubai International Airport

Dubai International Airport is the main gateway to the city and sits just 4 kilometres east of downtown. It is served by the Dubai Metro Red Line, with a 15‑minute ride to the city centre. A dedicated tram line runs from the airport to the Al Seef area and the Dubai Creek.

Buses, taxis and private hire vehicles are also readily available. The taxi fare to the city centre is typically between AED 30 and AED 45, depending on traffic and the exact destination.

Although Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC) is another major airport in the emirate, it is located 30 kilometres south of the city and is largely used for cargo and low‑cost carriers, making it less convenient for most travellers heading to central Dubai.

Finding the cheapest fares on the Mumbai‑Dubai route

To secure the best price, compare fares on a range of comparison sites and airline websites. Look for promotions from the main carriers, Emirates and Air India, as well as any discount codes that may be available.

Booking 6 to 8 weeks in advance typically yields the lowest fares, especially if you avoid weekends and peak holiday periods. Setting up price alerts can help you monitor fluctuations and book when a drop occurs.

Consider flexible travel dates; mid‑week flights are usually cheaper than weekend departures, and early‑morning or late‑night flights can offer additional savings.

Best time of year to travel and what to expect

The low season for travel between Mumbai and Dubai runs from January to March and from September to November. During these months the weather is warm but more comfortable, and fares are generally lower. The peak season, from December to February, coincides with the holiday period and Dubai’s winter attractions, so prices rise.

In terms of weather, Dubai is hot and dry throughout the year. The most pleasant period for sightseeing is from November to March, when temperatures are mild and humidity is low.

Crowd levels are highest during the winter months, so if you prefer quieter conditions, plan your trip for the shoulder seasons.

About Dubai – getting around and key attractions

Dubai’s public transport system is extensive. The Metro covers most of the city, while the tram and bus network provide access to areas not served by rail. For short distances, taxis and ride‑hailing services are convenient and inexpensive.

Major attractions include the Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, the historic Al Fahidi district, and the desert safari experience. The city also offers a range of cultural shows, water parks and theme parks that appeal to families.

If you have limited time, a guided city tour can help you see the highlights efficiently, but many visitors prefer to explore at their own pace using the Metro and walking routes.

Finding cheap flights from Mumbai to Dubai: frequently asked questions

How long is the flight from Mumbai to Dubai?

A direct flight from Mumbai to Dubai takes about 3 hours and 30 minutes. The two cities are 1,926 kilometres apart, and most airlines operate non‑stop services that maintain this average duration. Wind conditions and airport traffic can add a few minutes, but the typical flight time remains under four hours.

Which airlines fly from Mumbai to Dubai?

The main carriers on the Mumbai‑Dubai route are Emirates, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet and Flydubai. These airlines all offer non‑stop services from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport to Dubai International Airport. Occasionally, Air Arabia or other regional carriers operate seasonal flights, but the above airlines are the most reliable options.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Mumbai to Dubai?

Yes, every major airline on the route provides non‑stop flights from Mumbai (BOM) to Dubai (DXB). Passengers can choose from full‑service carriers such as Emirates and Air India or low‑cost options like IndiGo, SpiceJet and Flydubai, all of which operate direct services throughout the year.

How much does a flight from Mumbai to Dubai cost and what drives the price?

Economy fares for a one‑way ticket typically range from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000, depending on the carrier, booking time and season. Prices rise during peak holiday periods, festivals in India and the UAE, and when flights are booked close to departure. Low‑cost airlines offer cheaper fares but with fewer in‑flight services, while full‑service carriers charge a premium for extra comfort and amenities.

What is the cheapest month or time to fly from Mumbai to Dubai?

The lowest fares are usually found in January and February, when demand is lower after the Indian summer and before the Dubai holiday season. Late December, just before the New Year, can also offer competitive prices, especially if you book several weeks in advance and avoid the peak holiday period.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Mid‑week departures, particularly on Tuesday or Wednesday, tend to be cheaper because airlines often release discounted fares to fill seats. Early‑morning flights (before 9 am) or late‑evening flights (after 7 pm) also see lower prices, as they are less attractive for business travellers and can be booked at a discount.

How far in advance should I book flights from Mumbai to Dubai?

Booking 4 to 6 weeks before departure usually yields the best balance between price and availability for this route. For peak periods such as Diwali or the Dubai New Year celebrations, consider booking 8 to 10 weeks ahead to secure a lower fare and a preferred seat.

Which airports serve Mumbai and Dubai, and how far are they from the city centre?

Mumbai is served by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM), located about 24 km north of the city centre, with two main terminals. Dubai is served by Dubai International Airport (DXB), situated roughly 4 km east of downtown Dubai, and is a single‑terminal hub with three concourses for international traffic.

How many flights operate between Mumbai and Dubai?

There are typically 20 to 25 daily flights between Mumbai and Dubai, with a mix of full‑service and low‑cost carriers. The frequency peaks during the summer and winter holiday seasons, when demand from both Indian and UAE markets rises.

What is the flight distance between Mumbai and Dubai?

The great‑circle distance between Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport and Dubai International Airport is approximately 1,926 kilometres (1,198 miles). This distance translates into a flight time of about 3½ hours on a non‑stop service.

What is the best time of year to visit Dubai, and what is the weather like?

Dubai’s most pleasant weather occurs from November to March, when temperatures range from 20 °C to 30 °C and humidity is low. This period is ideal for outdoor activities, shopping festivals and desert safaris. The summer months (June to September) can exceed 40 °C, making indoor attractions and beach resorts the preferred choice.

How do I get the cheapest flight from Mumbai to Dubai?

To secure a low fare, use fare‑alert tools and compare prices across airlines, including low‑cost carriers. Book mid‑week, avoid peak holiday periods, and consider early‑morning or late‑evening flights. Signing up for airline newsletters and checking discount codes can also reduce the cost. Finally, be flexible with travel dates and allow for a few days’ buffer on either side of your preferred window.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo and Flydubai generally offer the lowest fares on the Mumbai‑Dubai route. The trade‑off is that these carriers provide basic seating, limited in‑flight entertainment and fewer complimentary services. Passengers seeking extra legroom, onboard meals or lounge access may prefer Air India or Emirates, which charge a premium for a more comfortable experience.

What makes Dubai International Airport a major hub for connecting flights from Mumbai?

Dubai International Airport (DXB) is a key global hub, especially for Emirates, which operates a vast network of flights to Europe, Asia, Africa and the Americas. From DXB, passengers can connect to over 200 destinations worldwide, making it an attractive transfer point for travellers from Mumbai who wish to reach further destinations in a single journey.
