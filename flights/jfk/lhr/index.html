Travelling from New York to London: what you need to know

  • Depart from John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) in New York.
  • Arrive at Heathrow Airport (LHR) in London.
  • The average non‑stop flight time is about 7 hours.
  • London is UTC+0, New York is UTC‑5, so there is a 5‑hour time difference.
  • The busiest pairing is JFK–LHR, with the majority of flights operated by British Airways.
  • Allow at least 2 hours before an international departure from JFK, and 3 hours in peak season or if you need to check a large carry‑on.

How to find the cheapest flights from New York to London

  • Book 6–8 weeks in advance to secure the lowest fares.
  • The cheapest months to fly are May, September and October, when demand is lower.
  • Set up a price alert on a comparison site so you are notified when fares drop.
  • Use the filter to sort results by cheapest, then by shortest flight time if you prefer a quicker journey.

Flying from New York: John F. Kennedy International Airport

  • Airlines offering non‑stop service include British Airways, American Airlines, United Airlines, Delta Air Lines and Virgin Atlantic.
  • Common one‑stop hubs for connecting flights are Newark (EWR), Charlotte (CLT) and Washington (IAD).
  • JFK is around 21 km from Manhattan; a car journey takes 45–60 minutes, public transport (subway + bus) 60–90 minutes.
  • The airport has six terminals, with the international departures mainly in Terminals 1, 4, 5 and 6.

Arriving in London: Heathrow Airport

  • Heathrow is about 23 km from central London.
  • By car the journey to Paddington takes 40–60 minutes, depending on traffic.
  • The Heathrow Express runs every 15 minutes and reaches Paddington in 15 minutes.
  • The Piccadilly line of the London Underground departs from Terminal 5 every 10 minutes, reaching Oxford Circus in 45 minutes.
  • Once in the city, the tube, buses and taxis provide comprehensive coverage.

Best time to go to London

  • July is the most popular month for visitors.
  • October offers a quieter, still pleasant experience and often cheaper accommodation.
  • Summer temperatures range from 20 °C to 27 °C; winter temperatures range from 3 °C to 10 °C.
  • Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) provide mild weather and fewer crowds.

More about London

  • Key sights include Buckingham Palace, the Tower of London, the British Museum and the National Gallery.
  • The Covent Garden area is famed for street performers, boutiques and theatres.
  • The South Bank hosts the London Eye, Shakespeare’s Globe and the Tate Modern.
  • The city’s neighbourhoods such as Notting Hill, Shoreditch and Greenwich each offer distinct character.

Explore more of the United Kingdom

  • Manchester is 200 km north of London; a direct train takes about 2 hours.
  • Edinburgh lies 650 km to the north‑east; the fastest train journey is 4½ hours.
  • Birmingham is 160 km west; the train ride takes roughly 1½ hours.
  • Each city boasts its own attractions: the Museum of Science and Industry in Manchester, the Royal Mile in Edinburgh, and the Bullring shopping centre in Birmingham.

Finding cheap flights from New York to London: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from New York JFK to London Heathrow?

To find cheap flights from New York JFK to London Heathrow, search for flexible dates, book 6–8 weeks in advance, and compare fares across airlines such as British Airways, Virgin Atlantic, American Airlines, Delta, and United. Avoid peak periods like Christmas and summer. Check early‑morning or late‑night departures, and consider nearby airports like Newark (EWR) for additional options. Use fare alerts on this page to track price drops.

What is the cheapest way to fly from New York JFK to London Heathrow?

The cheapest way to fly from New York JFK to London Heathrow is to book a trans‑Atlantic economy ticket with a low‑cost carrier such as Virgin Atlantic or British Airways when they offer promotional fares, or to use a connecting flight through a European hub like Dublin or Reykjavik. Look for weekday departures, avoid peak holidays, and book 6–8 weeks ahead. Compare fares on this page and set alerts to catch price reductions.

When is the best time to book a flight from New York JFK to London Heathrow to get the lowest price?

The best time to book a flight from New York JFK to London Heathrow for the lowest price is typically 6–8 weeks before departure, especially during the off‑peak season from late September to early March. Booking during this window often yields fares 10–20% cheaper than last‑minute tickets, and airlines release early‑bird discounts around this period.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from New York JFK to London Heathrow?

The cheapest months to fly from New York JFK to London Heathrow are January, February, and March, with late September to November also offering lower fares. These periods avoid the high demand of Christmas, New Year, and summer holidays, and airlines typically lower prices to fill seats. Prices can drop by 15–25% compared to peak summer rates.

How long is the flight from New York JFK to London Heathrow?

The flight from New York JFK to London Heathrow typically lasts between 7 hours and 10 minutes and 7 hours and 45 minutes, depending on wind conditions and the chosen airline. The average duration is about 7 hours and 30 minutes, covering a distance of 5,540 kilometres.

Which airlines fly from New York JFK to London Heathrow?

Airlines that operate flights from New York JFK to London Heathrow include British Airways, Virgin Atlantic, American Airlines, Delta Air Lines, United Airlines, Air France, Lufthansa, and Scandinavian carriers such as SAS. Low‑cost options like Norwegian used to serve the route, but currently the main carriers are the ones listed above, offering both nonstop and one‑stop services.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from New York JFK to London Heathrow?

Yes, there are direct non‑stop flights from New York JFK to London Heathrow operated by British Airways, Virgin Atlantic, American Airlines, Delta Air Lines, United Airlines, and Air France. These carriers provide daily nonstop services, with most flights departing in the evening from New York and arriving in the morning in London.

How much does a flight from New York JFK to London Heathrow usually cost / what drives the price?

A typical economy ticket from New York JFK to London Heathrow ranges from £250 to £600, depending on season, booking window, and airline. Prices rise during Christmas, summer, and major UK events, while off‑peak periods see lower fares. Factors such as baggage allowance, seat selection, and in‑flight services also influence the final cost. Low‑cost carriers often charge extra for checked bags and meals.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Midweek departures, especially on Tuesdays or Wednesdays, usually offer the lowest fares from New York JFK to London Heathrow. Early‑morning flights (before 8 am New York time) and late‑night flights (after 10 pm) can also be cheaper, as airlines schedule fewer passengers for those times. Avoid Friday and Sunday evenings, which tend to be the most expensive.

Which airports serve London (and New York), and how far are they from the city centre?

London is served by Heathrow Airport (LHR), about 23 km (14 miles) west of central London, and by Gatwick (Gatwick Airport) 45 km south of the city. New York is served by John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK), 24 km (15 miles) southeast of Manhattan, and by LaGuardia (LGA) 12 km (7 miles) north of Midtown. Both airports have extensive public‑transport links to their city centres.

How many flights operate between New York JFK and London Heathrow, and what is the distance?

There are roughly 30–35 nonstop flights per day between New York JFK and London Heathrow, operated by airlines such as British Airways, Virgin Atlantic, American Airlines, Delta, United, and Air France. The great‑circle distance between the two airports is about 5,540 km (3,450 miles), making it one of the longest trans‑Atlantic routes in the US–UK corridor.

What is the best time of year to visit London, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit London is during late spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October), when temperatures average 12–18 °C, rainfall is moderate, and tourist crowds are smaller than in summer. Winter months are cooler, with temperatures around 5–8 °C, while summer can reach 25 °C or higher. The city enjoys a mild maritime climate throughout the year.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

On the New York JFK to London Heathrow route, the cheapest fares are often offered by low‑cost carriers such as Virgin Atlantic or British Airways when they run promotional deals, but the trade‑off is limited baggage allowance and no complimentary meals or entertainment. Full‑service airlines like British Airways, Virgin Atlantic, and Air France provide free checked bags, seat selection, and in‑flight meals, but at a higher price point.

What is the best way to get from Heathrow Airport to central London?

The quickest way to travel from Heathrow Airport to central London is the Heathrow Express train, which takes 15 minutes to Paddington and costs around £22 one‑way. Alternatively, the Piccadilly Line of the London Underground runs every 10 minutes and costs £5.90, while a bus or taxi takes 45–60 minutes depending on traffic. All options connect to major city hubs.
