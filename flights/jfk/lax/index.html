Travelling from New York to Los Angeles: what you need to know

  • Depart from John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK), the busiest New York gateway with six terminals.
  • Arrive at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), the primary gateway to Southern California with a single, sprawling terminal complex.
  • The average non‑stop flight time is around six hours, covering roughly 3 974 km.
  • New York operates on Eastern Time (UTC‑5/‑4) and Los Angeles on Pacific Time (UTC‑8/‑7), a three‑hour difference.
  • JFK‑LAX is the busiest airport‑to‑airport pairing on this domestic corridor.
  • Aim to be at the departure gate at least two hours before take‑off, allowing extra time in peak summer and holiday periods.

How to find the cheapest flights from New York to Los Angeles

  • Book between six and eight weeks in advance for the best balance of price and seat selection.
  • Historically, January and February deliver the lowest fares, especially mid‑week departures.
  • Set a price alert on a comparison site to be notified of drops; most alerts trigger within a few days of a fare change.
  • Use the “cheapest” or “fastest” sort filters, then refine by airline if you have a loyalty programme.
  • Consider flexible dates; flying on Tuesdays or Wednesdays often reduces the ticket cost.

Flying from New York: John F. Kennedy International Airport

  • Non‑stop carriers include American Airlines, Delta Air Lines, United Airlines, Alaska Airlines and JetBlue Airways.
  • Common one‑stop hubs for this route are Chicago O'Hare (ORD), Dallas/Fort Worth (DFW) and Denver (DEN).
  • JFK sits about 24 km from Manhattan; a taxi or rideshare takes roughly 45 minutes in light traffic.
  • The AirTrain links to the subway (E, J, Z lines) and costs $8; the total public‑transport journey to the terminal is about 60 minutes.

Arriving in Los Angeles: Los Angeles International Airport

  • LAX is roughly 30 km southwest of downtown Los Angeles; a car or rideshare usually needs 30‑45 minutes, longer during rush hour.
  • The FlyAway Bus runs every 15‑30 minutes to Union Station, Hollywood, and Santa Monica, with a journey time of 45‑60 minutes.
  • Metro Bus Line 102 and the new LAX‑Metro Rail link (expected 2024) provide public‑transport options.
  • Once in the city, rideshare, rental cars and the Metro Rail system are the main ways to get around.

Best time to go to Los Angeles

  • The most popular month is August, when school holidays drive visitor numbers.
  • For lower crowds and better deals, visit in January or February; the weather remains mild.
  • Summer temperatures typically range from 18 °C to 28 °C, while winter averages sit between 8 °C and 20 °C.

More about Los Angeles

  • Iconic attractions include the Griffith Observatory, the Getty Center and the historic Hollywood Walk of Fame.
  • Beach districts such as Santa Monica and Venice offer boardwalks, bike paths and sunset views.
  • Cultural neighbourhoods like Little Tokyo, Koreatown and the Arts District showcase diverse food and street art.

Explore more of California

  • San Diego lies about 190 km south of Los Angeles; its Balboa Park and Pacific Beach are major draws.
  • San Francisco is roughly 560 km north; the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz and Fisherman’s Wharf attract millions.
  • Sacramento, the state capital, sits around 620 km northeast and features the historic Old Sacramento district and the California State Capitol Museum.

Finding cheap flights from New York to Los Angeles: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from New York to Los Angeles?

Search for economy fares on this page and set price alerts for the New York (JFK) – Los Angeles (LAX) route. Use flexible dates and look at departures a few days before or after your preferred day, because mid‑week flights are often cheaper. The cheapest months are January, February and September to early November, so plan travel then if possible. Consider nearby airports such as Newark (EWR) or LaGuardia (LGA) for New York and Burbank (BUR) or Long Beach (LGB) for Los Angeles, as they sometimes host lower‑priced services.

What is the cheapest way to fly from New York to Los Angeles?

The most economical way to travel from New York (JFK) to Los Angeles (LAX) is to book an economy ticket on a low‑cost carrier, travel on a Tuesday or Wednesday, and avoid peak holiday periods. Booking 6‑8 weeks in advance and using fare‑alert tools on this page can capture the lowest published fares. Connecting flights via a hub such as Dallas/Fort Worth (DFW) or Chicago O'Hare (ORD) sometimes undercut direct prices, though they add travel time.

When is the best time to book a flight from New York to Los Angeles to get the lowest price?

The optimal booking window for a New York (JFK) – Los Angeles (LAX) flight is roughly 70 days before departure, with most travellers finding the lowest fares when they book six to eight weeks ahead. Monitoring prices on this page for two to three months before travel and setting a price‑alert will usually capture the best deal, especially if you remain flexible on exact travel dates.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from New York to Los Angeles?

January, February and the period from September through early November are typically the cheapest months to fly from New York (JFK) to Los Angeles (LAX). During these off‑peak windows demand is lower, airlines run promotional fares and the average economy price drops by 15‑30 % compared with the summer months of June to August, when travel volume and prices peak.

How long is the flight from New York to Los Angeles?

A non‑stop flight between New York (JFK) and Los Angeles (LAX) covers approximately 3 974 kilometres and usually lasts between five hours thirty minutes and six hours, depending on wind conditions and the specific aircraft type. Most airlines schedule the flight for about six hours, allowing for standard taxi‑out and taxi‑in times at both busy international terminals.

Which airlines fly from New York to Los Angeles?

Major carriers operating between New York (JFK) and Los Angeles (LAX) include American Airlines, Delta Air Lines, United Airlines, JetBlue Airways, Alaska Airlines, Spirit Airlines and Frontier Airlines. These airlines provide a mix of non‑stop and one‑stop services, with American, Delta and United offering the highest frequency of daily flights, while JetBlue and Alaska add additional options during peak travel periods.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from New York to Los Angeles?

Yes, several airlines run direct, non‑stop services between New York’s John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) and Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) throughout the day. American Airlines, Delta Air Lines, United Airlines, JetBlue and Alaska Airlines all operate multiple daily non‑stop flights, giving travellers a wide choice of departure times without the need for a connecting hub.

How much does a flight from New York to Los Angeles usually cost and what drives the price?

Economy fares on the New York (JFK) – Los Angeles (LAX) route typically range from US$150 to US$600, with the lower end appearing during off‑peak months and the higher end during summer holidays or major events. Prices are driven by demand cycles, how far in advance you book, day‑of‑week trends, fuel costs, competition among airlines and any additional fees for baggage or seat selection on low‑cost carriers.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Travelling on a Tuesday or Wednesday generally yields the cheapest fares for the New York (JFK) to Los Angeles (LAX) corridor, especially on early‑morning departures before 08:00 UTC or late‑evening flights after 20:00 UTC. Mid‑week flights avoid the higher business‑travel demand of Monday and Friday, while early‑morning and late‑night slots often have lower load factors, prompting airlines to discount those seats.

Which airports serve Los Angeles (and New York), and how far are they from the city centre?

Los Angeles is served primarily by Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), located about 30 kilometres southwest of downtown. Secondary airports include Hollywood Burbank (BUR) roughly 20 kilometres north of the city centre and Long Beach Airport (LGB) about 30 kilometres southeast. New York’s main gateway is John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK), situated approximately 24 kilometres southeast of Manhattan; LaGuardia (LGA) lies 13 kilometres east, and Newark Liberty International (EWR) is about 16 kilometres west of the city.

How many flights operate between New York and Los Angeles, and what is the distance?

On an average day, around 15 to 20 non‑stop flights connect New York’s JFK airport with Los Angeles LAX, operated by the major U.S. carriers. The great‑circle distance between the two airports is roughly 3 974 kilometres (2 470 miles), making it one of the longest domestic routes in the United States and a key market for daily airline schedules.

What is the best time of year to visit Los Angeles, and what is the weather like?

The most pleasant periods to visit Los Angeles are April to June and September to October, when daytime temperatures average 18‑27 °C (65‑80 °F) and humidity is low. Summer can be warm, with highs often exceeding 30 °C, while winter brings mild days around 15 °C and occasional rain. These shoulder‑season months also coincide with lower hotel rates and fewer crowds at popular attractions.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

Spirit Airlines and Frontier Airlines usually offer the lowest base fares on the New York (JFK) – Los Angeles (LAX) route, often starting below US$150 for a basic economy seat. The trade‑off is that these carriers charge extra for checked baggage, carry‑on bags, seat selection and onboard refreshments, and they operate a tighter seat‑pitch configuration, which can affect comfort on the six‑hour flight.

What major attraction can I visit quickly after landing at Los Angeles International Airport?

The Santa Monica Pier is the closest iconic attraction to Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), situated roughly 30 kilometres north along the Pacific coastline. A short rideshare or shuttle ride of about 30 minutes will bring you to the pier, where you can enjoy the historic carousel, ocean‑front restaurants and the famous Pacific Park amusement area, making it an ideal first stop after a long flight.
