Finding cheap flights from Bengaluru to Chennai: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Bengaluru to Chennai?

Look for flights between BLR and MAA, use flexible dates, book 2‑3 months ahead, avoid peak holiday periods, consider nearby airports like Bangalore's Kempegowda International Airport and Chennai International Airport, check airlines like IndiGo, Air India Express, Bluebird. The most frequent carrier is IndiGo, which offers up to 12 daily flights. Prices are usually lower on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and booking between the 15th and 20th of a month can secure better rates.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Bengaluru to Chennai?

The cheapest way is to book a low‑cost carrier such as IndiGo or Air India Express, travel during off‑peak months, choose early‑morning or late‑evening slots, and book 2–3 months in advance. Avoid weekend travel, as fares rise during the weekend, and consider booking mid‑week to secure the lowest price.

When is the best time to book a flight from Bengaluru to Chennai to get the lowest price?

The best time to book is 2–3 months before departure, especially if you book between the 15th and 20th of a month, as fares tend to rise closer to the travel date. Checking fares 45 to 60 days ahead often yields the lowest rates, and setting a price alert on this page can notify you when a fare drops to its lowest point.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Bengaluru to Chennai?

The cheapest months are October to March, with October, November, and February typically offering the lowest fares, while July and August see higher prices due to monsoon travel. During the winter season (December to February) you can often find fares as low as ₹350, whereas the peak summer months (June to August) can push prices above ₹800.

How long is the flight from Bengaluru to Chennai?

The flight from BLR to MAA takes about 65 minutes on average, covering a distance of 268 km. Both airports operate in a single time zone (Indian Standard Time), so there is no time difference to account for when planning your arrival or departure. Typical in‑flight services include complimentary water and snacks, and the cabin crew offers a short safety briefing before takeoff.

Which airlines fly from Bengaluru to Chennai?

IndiGo, Air India Express, and Bluebird Airways operate daily services between Kempegowda International Airport and Chennai International Airport. IndiGo is the most frequent carrier, offering up to 12 flights per day, while Air India Express and Bluebird provide 4–6 daily options each. All three airlines provide standard economy seating with a 1.5‑meter seat pitch and a 7‑inch screen for in‑flight entertainment.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Bengaluru to Chennai?

Yes, all three carriers offer direct, non‑stop flights between BLR and MAA, with a typical duration of just over an hour. IndiGo runs up to 12 daily services, Air India Express offers 4 flights, and Bluebird operates 2–3 per day, all departing from Terminal 2 at Kempegowda and Terminal 3 at Chennai. These flights are scheduled throughout the day, with early‑morning departures around 06:00 and late‑evening arrivals around 20:00.

How much does a flight from Bengaluru to Chennai usually cost / what drives the price?

Economy fares normally range from ₹300 to ₹800, depending on booking window, day of week, and airline; price is driven by demand peaks, holiday periods, and fuel costs. During off‑peak months such as October and November, you can often secure fares near ₹350, whereas peak summer and festival periods can push prices above ₹900. Setting a price alert on this page will notify you when a fare drops to its lowest point.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Mid‑week flights, especially on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and early‑morning or late‑evening departures, tend to be cheaper than weekend or midday flights. Flights departing between 05:00 and 07:00 or arriving after 18:00 typically offer the lowest fares, as airlines aim to fill seats during less popular time slots. Avoid booking on Fridays or Saturdays, as fares rise by up to 20% during the weekend.

Which airports serve Chennai (and Bengaluru), and how far are they from the city centre?

Bengaluru is served by Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), about 30 km from the city centre, while Chennai is served by Chennai International Airport (MAA), roughly 12 km from the city centre. Both airports are well connected to downtown via metro, bus, and taxi services, with travel times of 45 minutes from BLR and 25 minutes from MAA during off‑peak hours.

How many flights operate between Bengaluru and Chennai, and what is the distance?

There are about 26 daily flights between BLR and MAA, covering a straight‑line distance of 268 km. Flights are operated by IndiGo, Air India Express, and Bluebird Airways, with IndiGo running the most frequent schedule of up to 12 departures per day. The average flight time is 65 minutes, and the route is serviced by both airlines' standard economy cabins.

What is the best time of year to visit Chennai, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Chennai is from November to February, when temperatures range from 20°C to 30°C and rainfall is minimal; the monsoon season from June to September brings heavy showers. During this dry period, you can enjoy beach resorts, temple festivals, and cultural events, while the cooler evenings make sightseeing comfortable. If you prefer lower prices, consider traveling in March or October, when fares dip but the weather remains pleasant.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo typically offers the lowest fares on the BLR–MAA route, but passengers may face stricter baggage limits and fewer in‑flight amenities compared to Air India Express and Bluebird. Air India Express provides a slightly higher price but includes a 10‑kg checked bag and complimentary snacks, while Bluebird offers the most basic service at the lowest cost.

What is the best way to reach Kempegowda International Airport from downtown Bengaluru?

The most convenient way to reach Kempegowda International Airport from downtown Bengaluru is to take the Namma Metro Purple Line to the airport station, which takes about 45 minutes and costs a few rupees, or use a pre‑booked taxi or app‑based ride for a direct 30‑minute journey. Alternatively, the city bus route 5B connects the city centre to the airport, taking roughly 60 minutes during peak traffic, but it is slower and less comfortable.
