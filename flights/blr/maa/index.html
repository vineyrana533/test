Overview of the Bengaluru‑Chennai route

The 268 km hop between Bengaluru’s Kempegowda International Airport and Chennai International Airport is one of India’s busiest domestic corridors. Flights are short, usually under an hour, and operate from early morning until late evening, giving travellers flexibility for business or leisure. Both airports are modern, with multiple terminals, ample retail, and efficient security, making the journey smooth even at peak times. The route is served by several low‑cost carriers and legacy airlines, providing a range of fare options and frequent departures throughout the week.

Kempegowda International Airport and getting to/from Bengaluru

Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) lies about 40 km north of Bengaluru’s city centre. The airport has two terminals – Terminal 1 for domestic low‑cost carriers and Terminal 2 for full‑service airlines. The Bangalore Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC) runs the Vayu Vajra airport shuttle (air‑conditioned, 24‑hour service) from Kempegowda Bus Station to the airport in roughly 90 minutes, depending on traffic. Taxis, app‑based rides and private hire cars are also readily available; the journey typically takes 60‑80 minutes. For those driving, the airport is accessed via NH 44 and NH 75, with ample short‑term and long‑term parking.

Chennai International Airport and getting to/from Chennai

Chennai International Airport (MAA) is situated about 22 km south of the city’s central business district. It comprises three terminals – one for domestic flights, one for international services and a newer terminal handling both. The Metropolitan Transport Corporation operates the MTC 400 series air‑conditioned bus service from various points in the city, reaching the airport in 45‑60 minutes. Auto‑rickshaws and app‑based taxis are plentiful; a typical ride from Marina Beach to the airport takes around 35 minutes. The airport is well‑connected to the Chennai Metro’s Blue Line via a shuttle service at the domestic terminal.

Finding the cheapest fares

To secure the lowest price on the Bengaluru‑Chennai sector, start by checking fare comparison sites early in the week, as many airlines release discounts on Tuesdays. Set price alerts for the route and be flexible with travel dates – departing on a Wednesday or Thursday often yields cheaper tickets. Booking 4‑6 weeks ahead captures the sweet spot between early‑bird discounts and last‑minute price hikes. Consider mixing carriers; for example, a low‑cost IndiGo outbound and a full‑service Air India return can reduce overall cost while still offering convenient timings.

Best time of year to travel and what to expect

The climate in both cities is tropical, with hot summers (April‑June) and a monsoon season (October‑December). The most pleasant period for flying is from November to February, when temperatures hover around 22‑28 °C and humidity is lower. During the festive season – especially around Diwali and Pongal – demand spikes, leading to higher fares and busier airports. Conversely, the pre‑monsoon months of March and early April see lighter crowds and the lowest price points. Regardless of season, the short flight duration means you’ll spend most of your time on the ground rather than in the air.

Getting around Chennai and top highlights

Chennai’s public transport includes a comprehensive bus network, the expanding Chennai Metro, and suburban rail services that link the airport to the city centre. For quick trips, app‑based taxis are reliable and reasonably priced. Must‑see attractions include Marina Beach, the historic Fort St. George, Kapaleeshwarar Temple in Mylapore, and the Government Museum. Food lovers should explore the bustling T. Nagar market for South Indian delicacies. The city’s compact layout makes it easy to combine sightseeing with business meetings, and the efficient rail links mean you can reach most attractions within 30‑45 minutes from the airport.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Bengaluru to Chennai?

April, May, September and October are generally the cheapest months to fly between Bengaluru (BLR) and Chennai (MAA) because demand is lower after the summer holidays and before the winter festive rush. During these shoulder‑season periods airlines run promotional fares and load factors are lower, which pushes the average price down by roughly 20‑25 % compared with peak months like December and January.

How long is the flight from Bengaluru to Chennai?

A non‑stop flight between Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) and Chennai International Airport (MAA) takes about one hour and ten minutes, covering a distance of roughly 268 kilometres. The short duration is due to the close proximity of the two cities on the east coast of southern India, and most airlines schedule the flight to depart and arrive within a tight one‑hour window, making it the fastest way to travel compared with a six‑hour road journey.

Which airlines fly from Bengaluru to Chennai?

IndiGo, SpiceJet, Air India, Vistara, GoFirst and Air India Express operate scheduled services between Bengaluru (BLR) and Chennai (MAA). These carriers provide a mix of low‑cost and full‑service options, with multiple daily departures throughout the day. IndiGo and SpiceJet dominate the market with the highest frequency, while Air India and Vistara offer a few premium‑service flights that include complimentary meals and checked baggage.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Bengaluru to Chennai?

Yes, there are numerous direct, non‑stop flights each day between Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) and Chennai International Airport (MAA). All of the airlines listed above operate at least one nonstop service, and the combined schedule provides roughly 15‑20 departures daily, ensuring you can travel without a layover at any time of the day.

How much does a flight from Bengaluru to Chennai usually cost and what drives the price?

Economy fares on low‑cost carriers typically range from INR 3,000 to INR 8,000 for a one‑way ticket, while full‑service airlines may charge between INR 7,000 and INR 12,000. Prices are driven by factors such as booking lead time, travel dates (peak holidays increase fares), time of day, airline competition on the route and the inclusion of extras like checked baggage or meals. What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Tuesday and Wednesday are generally the cheapest days to depart from Bengaluru to Chennai, and flights that leave early in the morning around 06:00 local time or late at night after 22:00 often have the lowest base fares. Mid‑week, off‑peak departures avoid business‑travel surcharges and result in price reductions of up to 15 % compared with Friday or Sunday midday flights.

Which airports serve Chennai and Bengaluru, and how far are they from the city centre?

Bengaluru is served by Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), which lies about 40 kilometres north of the city centre and is reachable via the NH 44 highway or a dedicated airport express service. Chennai is served by Chennai International Airport (MAA), situated roughly 13 kilometres south of the central business district and connected by the Chennai Suburban Railway, MTC buses and a fast‑track airport shuttle.

How many flights operate between Bengaluru and Chennai, and what is the distance?

Approximately 15‑20 scheduled flights operate each day between BLR and MAA, covering a straight‑line distance of 268 kilometres. The high frequency is provided by multiple carriers, ensuring at least an hourly departure during peak periods and a few early‑morning and late‑night options for travellers seeking flexibility.

What is the best time of year to visit Chennai, and what is the weather like?

The most pleasant time to visit Chennai is between October and March, when daytime temperatures range from 20 °C to 30 °C and humidity is moderate, making outdoor activities comfortable. The monsoon season from June to September brings heavy rain and higher humidity, while April and May can be hot, with temperatures often exceeding 35 °C. Planning your trip in the winter months also aligns with the cheapest flight periods for the Bengaluru‑Chennai route.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo and SpiceJet usually offer the lowest base fares on the Bengaluru‑Chennai sector, often under INR 4,000 for a one‑way economy ticket. The trade‑off is that these low‑cost carriers typically charge extra for checked baggage, seat selection and onboard meals, and their fare rules may be more restrictive regarding changes or refunds. If you travel light and can be flexible, the savings can outweigh the additional fees.

How does the flight time from Bengaluru to Chennai compare with travelling by road?

A non‑stop flight between BLR and MAA takes about 1 hour 10 minutes, whereas the same journey by road on the NH 48 covers roughly 350 kilometres and usually requires 6‑7 hours, depending on traffic and weather. Flying therefore saves around five to six hours of travel time, making it the preferred option for business travellers and tourists who want to maximise their time in either city.
