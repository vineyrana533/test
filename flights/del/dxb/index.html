Travelling from Delhi to Dubai: what you need to know

  • Delhi’s main international gateway is Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), with three terminals and a distance of about 32 km from the city centre.
  • Dubai International Airport (DXB) is the destination, also with three terminals, situated roughly 15 km from downtown Dubai.
  • The average nonstop flight takes around 3 hours 30 minutes.
  • Delhi is in IST (+5:30) and Dubai in GST (+4:00), a difference of 1 ½ hours behind.
  • The most frequent pairing is DEL‑DXB, with Emirates and IndiGo operating the bulk of the traffic.
  • Allow at least 2 hours before an international departure, especially in peak season.

How to find the cheapest flights from Delhi to Dubai

  • Book 3–6 months in advance to secure the lowest fares.
  • The cheapest month to fly is typically June or July, when demand is lower.
  • Set up a price alert on a flight‑comparison site to catch sudden drops.
  • Use the filter to sort by cheapest or fastest options, then compare the two.

Flying from Delhi: Indira Gandhi International Airport

  • Emirates, IndiGo and Air India operate nonstop services.
  • Common one‑stop connections include Mumbai (BOM) and Kolkata (CCU).
  • The airport sits 32 km from the city centre; a car journey takes 45–60 minutes, while the Airport Express metro line to Delhi station takes about 30 minutes.

Arriving in Dubai: Dubai International Airport

  • DXB is 15 km from downtown; a taxi ride takes 30–45 minutes.
  • Public transport options include the Dubai Metro (red line to Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall) and a network of buses.
  • Once in the city, the metro, tram, taxis and ride‑hailing apps provide convenient access to most attractions.

Best time to go to Dubai

  • December and January are the most popular months, with temperatures ranging from 15 °C to 25 °C.
  • The cheapest period is June and July, when temperatures climb to 30 °C–40 °C but demand is lower.
  • Summer can be very hot, while winter offers mild weather and lower humidity.

More about Dubai

  • Key sights include the Burj Khalifa, The Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina and the historic Al Fahidi district.
  • The city blends ultramodern architecture with cultural heritage, making it a diverse travel experience.

Explore more of UAE

  • Abu Dhabi is about 140 km (1.5 hours) from Dubai and hosts the Sheikh Zayed Mosque and Louvre Abu Dhabi.
  • Sharjah, 45 km away, offers the Sharjah Heritage Area and Al Noor Mosque.
  • Al Ain, 220 km away, is known for its historic forts and the Al Ain Zoo.

Booking 6–8 weeks in advance and using flexible dates on this page can reduce fares to the low‑£70 range. What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Delhi to Dubai?

The cheapest months to fly from Delhi to Dubai are January, February and March, when demand is low after the New Year and before the summer peak. Prices are usually 10–20 % lower than in the peak months of June, July and August. Avoid the Ramadan and Eid periods, which see a temporary spike in fares.

How long is the flight from Delhi to Dubai?

A typical flight from Delhi (DEL) to Dubai (DXB) takes about 4 hours 30 minutes, covering the 2,183 km distance between the two capitals. Flight times can vary by a few minutes depending on wind and air traffic.

Which airlines fly from Delhi to Dubai?

The Delhi–Dubai route is served by several airlines, including Emirates, IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Flydubai, Air Arabia and Air India Express. Emirates offers the most frequent service, while the low‑cost carriers provide cheaper alternatives.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Delhi to Dubai?

Yes, all airlines listed above operate non‑stop flights from Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) to Dubai International Airport (DXB).

How much does a flight from Delhi to Dubai usually cost / what drives the price?

A Delhi–Dubai flight usually costs between £70 and £200, depending on the carrier, booking window, seat class and season. Low‑cost airlines such as IndiGo and SpiceJet offer fares in the £70–£120 range during off‑season, while Emirates and Air India can charge £150–£200 in peak periods. Prices rise during Diwali, Eid and the summer holidays.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Mid‑week flights on Tuesday or Wednesday, departing between 9 am and 1 pm, generally offer the lowest fares on the Delhi–Dubai route. Early‑morning or late‑afternoon flights tend to be pricier due to higher demand.

Which airports serve Dubai (and Delhi), and how far are they from the city centre?

Delhi is served by Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), located about 32 km from the city centre. Dubai is served by Dubai International Airport (DXB), situated roughly 14 km from downtown Dubai. Both airports have dedicated rail links and bus services that connect to the city cores.

How many flights operate between Delhi and Dubai, and what is the distance?

There are roughly 10–12 non‑stop flights per day between Delhi and Dubai, covering the 2,183 km distance. The most frequent carrier, Emirates, operates around 8 flights daily, while the low‑cost airlines provide the remaining services.

What is the best time of year to visit Dubai, and what is the weather like?

Dubai is best visited between November and March when temperatures range from 20 °C to 25 °C and humidity is low. April to June can be hot, with highs of 35 °C to 40 °C, while July to October is very hot and humid, making it less comfortable for outdoor activities.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo and SpiceJet are the cheapest airlines on the Delhi–Dubai route, offering fares as low as £70–£90. The trade‑off is a smaller cabin, fewer in‑flight amenities and no lounge access, whereas Emirates and Air India provide full‑service comfort but at higher prices.

What is the main entry requirement for Indian passport holders traveling from Delhi to Dubai?

Indian passport holders travelling from Delhi to Dubai need a valid passport, a UAE e‑visa or visa on arrival, and a return or onward ticket. No vaccination certificate is required for entry at present, but travelers should check for any updated health requirements before departure.
