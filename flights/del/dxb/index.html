Overview of the Delhi‑Dubai Route

The flight from Delhi to Dubai covers approximately 2,183 kilometres and normally takes 3 hours and 30 minutes when flying direct. The route is one of the most popular in the Middle East, with several carriers offering daily services. Both cities operate in the same time zone offset of UTC+4, so the time difference is negligible. The high frequency of flights makes it easy to find a departure that suits your schedule, whether you prefer a morning or evening arrival in Dubai.

Getting to and from Indira Gandhi International Airport

Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) sits about 30 kilometres south of central Delhi. It has three terminals, with Terminal 3 handling most international traffic. The Delhi Metro’s Airport Express Line connects the airport to the city centre in roughly 45 minutes, and the airport is also served by a dedicated bus service, a plentiful taxi fleet and several ride‑hailing apps. For those driving, the airport offers ample parking and a dedicated car‑hire area. The main road link is the Delhi‑Gurgaon Expressway, which can be congested during peak hours.

Dubai International Airport and City Access

Dubai International Airport (DXB) is located just 5 kilometres from the city centre and is served by three terminals. The Dubai Metro’s Red Line stops at the airport, providing a quick 15‑minute ride into downtown. Regular bus routes, a dedicated airport bus service, taxis and ride‑hailing options are also available. For visitors arriving by car, the airport offers extensive parking facilities. The airport’s proximity to the city means you can reach most attractions within 20 minutes by metro or taxi.

Finding the Cheapest Fares on the Delhi‑Dubai Route

To secure the best price, compare fares across multiple platforms and keep an eye on fare‑alert services. Flexibility with travel dates can yield significant savings; mid‑week flights are often cheaper than weekend departures. Booking early—typically 3 to 6 weeks before travel—can lock in lower rates, especially for full‑service carriers. Low‑cost airlines such as IndiGo and Air Arabia frequently offer competitive fares, though they may charge extra for baggage and seat selection. Consider a short layover via Doha or Abu Dhabi if a direct flight is priced higher.

Best Time of Year to Travel

Dubai’s climate is hot and dry from May to September, with temperatures often exceeding 40°C. The most pleasant months are November to April, when temperatures range from 20°C to 30°C and humidity is lower. This period also coincides with the peak tourist season, so flights and accommodation can be pricier. If you prefer lower prices and can tolerate heat, late spring or early autumn offer a compromise. Book flights 2 to 4 months in advance for the most favourable fares during peak season.

Getting Around Dubai and Key Highlights

Dubai’s public transport network includes the metro, tram, and bus lines, all of which connect major tourist sites. Taxis are plentiful and reasonably priced, with a flat fare for airport transfers. For a unique experience, try a water taxi along the Jumeirah waterfront. Must‑see attractions include the Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, the historic Al Fahidi district, Palm Jumeirah, and the desert safari. The city also hosts a range of cultural events, such as the Dubai Shopping Festival in January and the Dubai International Film Festival in December.

Finding cheap flights from Delhi to Dubai: frequently asked questions

How long is the flight from Delhi to Dubai?

The flight from Delhi to Dubai takes roughly 3 hours and 40 minutes. The 2,183‑kilometre journey covers the airspace between Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) and Dubai International Airport (DXB). Most carriers schedule non‑stop services that average 3 h 30 m to 3 h 45 m, with a few flights offering a brief layover in nearby hubs. The route is popular for business and leisure, so flights are available throughout the day.

Which airlines fly from Delhi to Dubai?

A range of carriers operate Delhi–Dubai routes, including full‑service airlines Emirates, Air India, and low‑cost operators IndiGo, SpiceJet, flydubai and Air Arabia. Emirates is the most frequent, offering multiple daily flights with premium lounges. IndiGo and Air India provide the cheapest fares but with fewer amenities. Flydubai and Air Arabia offer budget options with a single‑class cabin. All services are non‑stop from Indira Gandhi International Airport to Dubai International Airport.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Delhi to Dubai?

The Delhi–Dubai route is served entirely by non‑stop flights. All airlines that operate on this corridor, such as Emirates, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, flydubai and Air Arabia, fly directly from Indira Gandhi International Airport to Dubai International Airport with no layovers. This makes the journey straightforward and convenient for business and leisure travellers alike.

How much does a flight from Delhi to Dubai cost / what drives the price?

Fares for Delhi–Dubai flights vary widely, typically ranging from £70 to £250 for a one‑way economy ticket, depending on the carrier, season and booking window. Low‑cost airlines such as IndiGo and Air India often offer the lowest prices, while Emirates and flydubai charge a premium for extra legroom and in‑flight services. Prices rise during peak holiday periods, the Indian summer (April–June) and the UAE's winter holidays (December–January).

What is the cheapest month/time to fly from Delhi to Dubai?

September through November are usually the cheapest months to fly from Delhi to Dubai. During this shoulder period, demand drops after the Indian monsoon and before the UAE's peak winter season, allowing airlines to offer lower fares. Early‑morning departures on weekdays often provide the best value, while flights booked in advance of the peak summer season (April–June) can also be reasonably priced.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

The most economical times to depart Delhi for Dubai are typically Wednesday or Thursday mornings between 6:00 and 9:00 am. Mid‑week flights avoid the higher demand of weekend travel, and early‑morning departures benefit from lower fuel costs and less congestion at the airport. Conversely, late‑night or weekend flights tend to carry higher fares, especially during holiday periods.

How far in advance should I book flights from Delhi to Dubai?

Booking 3 to 6 months ahead of your intended travel dates generally yields the best fares for Delhi–Dubai flights. Airlines release their schedules and discounted seats in early spring, so planning in March or April can secure lower prices. For last‑minute travel, booking within 2–3 weeks may still find competitive rates, but the risk of higher fares increases, especially during peak seasons.

Which airports serve Delhi and Dubai, and how far are they from the city centre?

Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) sits about 30 km north of Delhi's city centre, accessible by the Delhi Metro, taxis and private hire. Dubai International Airport (DXB) lies roughly 15 km south of downtown Dubai, with convenient metro, bus and taxi links. Both airports offer extensive ground‑transport options, including express trains and dedicated airport shuttles, making city access straightforward for most travellers.

How many flights operate between Delhi and Dubai?

On average, roughly 15 to 20 non‑stop flights operate each day between Delhi and Dubai, with a mix of full‑service and low‑cost carriers. The frequency peaks during the Indian summer (April–June) and the UAE's winter holiday season (December–January), when demand surges. Return flights are similarly frequent, providing travellers with flexible scheduling options.

What is the flight distance between Delhi and Dubai?

The straight‑line distance between Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) and Dubai International Airport (DXB) is approximately 2,183 kilometres (about 1,357 miles). This distance translates into a flight time of around 3 hours 30 minutes to 3 hours 45 minutes, depending on wind conditions and the specific carrier's routing.

What is the best time of year to visit Dubai, and what is the weather like?

Dubai is best visited during the cooler winter months from December to February, when daytime temperatures average 20–25 °C and humidity remains low. This period also coincides with many cultural festivals and the Dubai Shopping Festival. Summer (June–August) can see temperatures above 40 °C, making indoor attractions and early‑morning activities preferable. Spring (March–May) offers mild weather but can be wet during the monsoon season.

How do I get the cheapest flight from Delhi to Dubai?

To secure the cheapest Delhi–Dubai fare, compare prices on multiple comparison sites, set fare alerts, and remain flexible with travel dates. Booking mid‑week and avoiding peak holiday periods reduces costs. Opt for low‑cost carriers like IndiGo or Air India, but be aware of lower baggage limits and no in‑flight meals. Consider flying into Dubai International Airport and using the metro or bus for city access to avoid extra airport‑to‑city transfer fees.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

The cheapest option on the Delhi–Dubai route is usually IndiGo or Air India, offering one‑way economy fares as low as £70–£90. The trade‑off is minimal in‑flight entertainment, no complimentary meals, and a lower baggage allowance of 15 kg for carry‑on and 20 kg for checked luggage. Full‑service carriers like Emirates provide extra legroom, lounge access and in‑flight meals, but at a higher price point.

What are the visa requirements for Indian citizens traveling to Dubai?

Indian citizens travelling to Dubai for tourism or business need to obtain an e‑visa before departure. The online application requires a passport valid for at least six months, a recent passport‑size photo, and a return ticket. The e‑visa is usually processed within 48 hours and allows a 30‑day stay, renewable for an additional 30 days. Fees are paid online and the visa is attached to the passport electronically.
