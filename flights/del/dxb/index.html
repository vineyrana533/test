Travelling from Delhi to Dubai: what you need to know

  • Depart from Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), the sole international gateway in Delhi.
  • Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB), the main hub for international traffic in the UAE.
  • Average direct flight duration is about 4 hours 30 minutes.
  • Dubai is 1.5 hours behind Delhi (GST UTC+4 vs IST UTC+5:30).
  • The most frequent pairing is DEL–DXB, with several daily services.
  • Allow at least 2 hours before an international departure, more during peak season.

How to find the cheapest flights from Delhi to Dubai

  • Book 2–3 months in advance to secure the best fares.
  • The cheapest month to fly is generally January or February, when demand is lower.
  • Set up a price alert on a comparison site to catch drops.
  • Use the filter to sort by cheapest or by shortest duration to see the full range.

Flying from Delhi: Indira Gandhi International Airport

  • Non‑stop carriers include Emirates, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet and Flydubai.
  • Common one‑stop hubs are Doha (Qatar Airways), Abu Dhabi (Etihad) and Istanbul (Turkish Airlines).
  • The airport sits about 32 km north of Delhi’s city centre.
  • By car it takes roughly 1 hour; by the Airport Express or metro plus local transport about 1½–2 hours.

Arriving in Dubai: Dubai International Airport

  • DXB is only about 4 km from downtown Dubai.
  • A taxi takes 15–20 minutes; the metro (Red Line) takes 30 minutes to reach the city centre.
  • Once in the city, the metro, buses, taxis and water taxis offer convenient travel to popular districts.

Best time to go to Dubai

  • The peak tourist season is December to February, when temperatures are 20–25 °C.
  • A cheaper and quieter period is April to May, with summer temperatures 35–40 °C.
  • The city experiences mild winters and scorching summers, so choose the season that suits your tolerance for heat.

More about Dubai

  • Must‑see landmarks include the Burj Khalifa, The Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah and the historic Al Fahidi district.
  • The Dubai Marina and Jumeirah Beach offer modern leisure and nightlife.
  • Cultural sites such as the Sheikh Mohammed Centre for Cultural Understanding provide insight into Emirati heritage.

Explore more of the UAE

  • Abu Dhabi is about 140 km south of Dubai; visit the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and Louvre Abu Dhabi.
  • Sharjah, 30 km east, is known for its museums and the Al Noor Mosque.
  • Al Ain, roughly 230 km southeast, features the Al Ain Zoo and the Al Jahili Fort.

Finding cheap flights from Delhi to Dubai: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Delhi to Dubai?

Use flexible dates, book 6‑8 weeks ahead, check low-cost carriers like IndiGo, Air India Express, flydubai, and look for sales during January‑February or late March. Compare fares on this page, set alerts, and consider nearby airports like Dehradun or Lucknow for potential savings. The Delhi‑Dubai route is served by multiple airlines, so using a flexible search can uncover hidden discounts.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Delhi to Dubai?

The cheapest way is to fly with low-cost carriers such as IndiGo, Air India Express, or flydubai, booking early and using flexible dates. These airlines offer fares from around INR 4,000 to 6,000 for a one‑way ticket. Avoid peak holiday periods and choose mid‑week departures to maximise savings. Additionally, checking for promotional codes and airline newsletters can further reduce the cost.

When is the best time to book a flight from Delhi to Dubai to get the lowest price?

The best time to book is 6 to 8 weeks before departure, especially during the low‑season months of January, February and early November. Prices tend to rise as the travel date approaches, so booking in advance and monitoring price alerts on this page can secure the lowest fares. Also, avoid booking during major Indian festivals or UAE holidays when fares spike.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Delhi to Dubai?

The cheapest months are January, February and early November, when demand is lower and airlines run promotional fares. Late March also sees reduced prices after the peak winter holiday season. Avoid the Indian festival period of December‑January and the UAE summer months of June‑August when fares rise. Booking during these low‑demand windows often results in the most competitive fares.

How long is the flight from Delhi to Dubai?

The flight covers 2,183 kilometres and typically takes 3 hours 45 minutes to 4 hours, depending on wind and aircraft type. Direct flights operate daily, and most carriers schedule departures between 6 am and 8 pm. The average cruising speed is about 850 km/h, and the route is served by Airbus A320 and Boeing 737 families today.

Which airlines fly from Delhi to Dubai?

Direct airlines include Emirates, IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express, flydubai, and Air Arabia. These carriers operate multiple daily flights, offering a mix of full-service and low-cost options. Emirates provides premium cabins, while IndiGo and Air India Express offer budget fares. Other airlines such as Air India Limited and Etihad occasionally operate seasonal services today.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Delhi to Dubai?

Yes, several airlines operate direct, non‑stop flights from Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) to Dubai International Airport (DXB), including Emirates, IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express, flydubai and Air Arabia. These flights run throughout the day, providing convenient options for travellers. The average flight time is about 3 hours 45 minutes, and most airlines offer multiple departure slots between early morning and late evening.

How much does a flight from Delhi to Dubai usually cost / what drives the price?

One‑way fares typically range from INR 4,000 to 12,000, depending on the carrier, booking window, season and class. Low‑cost airlines such as IndiGo and Air India Express offer the lowest prices, while Emirates and Air Arabia charge higher for premium service. Prices rise during Indian festivals, UAE holidays and summer months, and are lower in January‑February and early November.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Mid‑week departures, especially Tuesdays and Wednesdays, usually yield the cheapest fares. Early‑morning flights (6–8 am) and late‑evening flights (8–10 pm) are also often cheaper than midday services. Booking 6–8 weeks ahead and monitoring price alerts on this page can further reduce costs. In addition, avoiding peak Indian holidays and UAE national days can help keep fares low.

Which airports serve Dubai (and Delhi), and how far are they from the city centre?

Delhi is served by Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), located about 30 km north of central Delhi, reachable via metro, taxi or bus. Dubai is served by Dubai International Airport (DXB), situated roughly 4 km south of the city centre; it is connected by the Dubai Metro, taxis, buses and the free Dubai Tram. Both airports offer extensive ground transport options, including dedicated airport shuttles and ride‑hailing services.

How many flights operate between Delhi and Dubai, and what is the distance?

There are typically 15 to 20 daily direct flights between Delhi and Dubai, operated by airlines such as Emirates, IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express, flydubai and Air Arabia. The straight‑line distance is 2,183 km, and the flight duration is about 3 hours 45 minutes to 4 hours. Typical departure times range from early morning to late evening, allowing travellers flexibility.

What is the best time of year to visit Dubai, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Dubai is between November and March, when temperatures range from 20 °C to 28 °C and humidity is low. This period offers comfortable weather for sightseeing, beach activities and desert tours. Summer months (June–August) can exceed 40 °C, making outdoor activities less pleasant. During the cooler months, indoor attractions such as museums and malls remain comfortably air‑conditioned, enhancing the overall experience.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

The cheapest airlines are IndiGo and Air India Express, offering fares as low as INR 4,000–6,000 for a one‑way ticket. The trade‑off is limited legroom, fewer in‑flight amenities and a basic cabin. Emirates provides a premium experience with seat‑back entertainment, meals and lounge access, but at a higher price. Choosing a low‑cost carrier saves money but may require additional baggage fees and limited seat selection.

What are the visa requirements for Indian passport holders traveling from Delhi to Dubai?

Indian passport holders need a pre‑arranged UAE tourist visa, which can be obtained online through the UAE e‑visa portal or via a travel agency. The visa is valid for 30 days, can be extended to 90 days, and costs around AED 100–120. The application requires a passport valid for at least six months, a recent photo, flight itinerary and proof of accommodation.
