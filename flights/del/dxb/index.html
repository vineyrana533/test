Travelling from Delhi to Dubai: what you need to know

  • Depart from Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), Delhi’s main international hub with three terminals.
  • Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB), the busiest airport in the UAE, also with three terminals.
  • Average non‑stop flight time is around 3 hours 45 minutes.
  • Dubai is 1 hour 30 minutes behind Delhi (IST UTC+5:30 vs GST UTC+4:00).
  • The DEL‑DXB pairing is the busiest India‑UAE corridor, with dozens of daily seats.
  • For an international departure, aim to be at the check‑in desk at least two hours before take‑off, three hours during peak holiday periods.

How to find the cheapest flights from Delhi to Dubai

  • Prices are usually lowest when you book 6–8 weeks in advance; last‑minute fares can be 30‑50 % higher.
  • September and early October are historically the cheapest months, avoiding both Indian school holidays and Dubai’s summer heat.
  • Set a price alert on a comparison site; the tool will email you when the fare drops.
  • Use the “cheapest” filter first, then sort by “shortest duration” to spot good‑value non‑stop options.

Flying from Delhi: Indira Gandhi International Airport

  • Emirates, Air India, IndiGo and Flydubai all operate non‑stop services from DEL to DXB.
  • Common one‑stop connections include Doha (DOH), Istanbul (IST) and Muscat (MCT) for carriers that do not fly direct.
  • The airport lies about 16 km (10 mi) from central Delhi; the Metro Airport Express takes roughly 20 minutes, while a taxi journey is 30–45 minutes depending on traffic.
  • Parking is available on‑site; short‑term rates start at INR 150 per hour, and long‑term rates are cheaper per day.

Arriving in Dubai: Dubai International Airport

  • DXB is situated about 14 km (9 mi) southeast of Dubai’s city centre.
  • The Dubai Metro Red Line reaches Terminal 3 in around 15 minutes; a taxi from the arrivals hall to downtown Dubai typically takes 15–20 minutes.
  • Public buses and the Dubai Tram also connect the airport to key districts such as Deira and Bur Dubai.
  • Once in the city, the Metro, taxis and ride‑hailing apps provide easy access to attractions across the emirate.

Best time to go to Dubai

  • November to March is the peak tourist season, with pleasant temperatures and the highest hotel occupancy.
  • May to September is the low‑season period; hotels and flights are cheaper, but daytime temperatures often exceed 40 °C.
  • Average summer highs range from 30 °C to 45 °C, while winter lows sit between 14 °C and 25 °C.
  • The most popular month for visitors is February, coinciding with the Dubai Shopping Festival.

More about Dubai

  • The Burj Khalifa, the world’s tallest building, offers observation decks with panoramic desert views.
  • The historic Al Fahidi neighbourhood (Al Bastakiya) showcases traditional wind‑tower architecture and art galleries.
  • Dubai Marina and Jumeirah Beach provide a mix of modern skyscrapers, waterfront dining and beach leisure.
  • For a cultural showcase, visit the Dubai Museum in Al Shindagha Fort and the Gold Souk in Deira.

Explore more of United Arab Emirates

  • Abu Dhabi, about 150 km southwest of Dubai, is home to the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and the Louvre Abu Dhabi.
  • Sharjah, roughly 30 km north, offers the Sharjah Arts Museum and the historic Al Noor Mosque.
  • Ras Al Khaimah, 180 km north‑east, is known for the Hajar Mountains and Jebel Jais, the UAE’s highest peak.

Finding cheap flights from Delhi to Dubai: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Delhi to Dubai?

To find cheap flights from Delhi (DEL) to Dubai (DXB) you should compare fares on this page, search with flexible dates, and include nearby airports such as Sharjah (SHJ) when relevant. Booking 6‑8 weeks in advance, travelling mid‑week, and using price‑alert tools on this page often reveal the lowest offers. Avoid peak holiday periods, and consider low‑cost carriers like IndiGo or SpiceJet, which may charge for extras but keep the base fare low.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Delhi to Dubai?

The cheapest way to fly from Delhi to Dubai is to use a low‑cost airline such as IndiGo, Spice Jet or Air India Express, book several weeks ahead, and travel on a Tuesday or Wednesday morning. Selecting a non‑peak travel window, for example late September to early November, and forgoing optional services like checked baggage or seat selection reduces the total cost. All fare options can be filtered and compared on this page.

When is the best time to book a flight from Delhi to Dubai to get the lowest price?

The best time to book a flight from Delhi to Dubai for the lowest price is typically 45‑60 days before departure, with a secondary dip around 90 days out for some carriers. Prices tend to rise sharply within two weeks of the travel date and during Indian festival periods such as Diwali. Monitoring price trends on this page and setting a fare alert will help you capture the optimal booking window.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Delhi to Dubai?

The cheapest month to fly from Delhi to Dubai is usually November, followed by September and October, when demand falls after the Indian summer holidays and before the winter tourism peak in the UAE. January and February see higher fares due to cooler weather in Dubai attracting tourists. Flying in these shoulder months typically yields fares 15‑30 % lower than peak summer or holiday periods.

How long is the flight from Delhi to Dubai?

The non‑stop flight from Delhi (Indira Gandhi International Airport, DEL) to Dubai (Dubai International Airport, DXB) takes approximately 3 hours and 30 minutes, covering a distance of about 2,183 kilometres. Flight time can vary by a few minutes depending on wind conditions and the specific aircraft, but most airlines schedule the journey within the three‑and‑a‑half hour window.

Which airlines fly from Delhi to Dubai?

Airlines that operate flights between Delhi and Dubai include Emirates, flydubai, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, Vistara and Air India Express. Emirates and flydubai provide full‑service options with meals and baggage, while IndiGo, SpiceJet and Air India Express offer low‑cost services. All carriers run multiple daily departures, giving travellers a range of departure times and cabin classes to choose from.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Delhi to Dubai?

Yes, there are direct non‑stop flights from Delhi’s Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) to Dubai International Airport (DXB) operated by Emirates, flydubai, Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet and Vistara. These flights typically last about 3 hours 30 minutes and run throughout the day, with early‑morning, midday and evening options available on most airlines.

How much does a flight from Delhi to Dubai usually cost and what drives the price?

A typical one‑way fare from Delhi to Dubai ranges from ₹8,000 to ₹25,000 (approximately $100‑$330), depending on airline, booking window and travel season. Low‑cost carriers sit at the lower end, while full‑service airlines charge more for included meals, baggage and flexible tickets. Prices rise during Indian holidays, UAE summer festivals and when seats are limited, and fall during shoulder months such as September‑November.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Flying on a Tuesday or Wednesday, especially on early‑morning departures before 09:00, usually yields the lowest fares on the Delhi‑Dubai route. Mid‑week demand is weaker, prompting airlines to discount seats, and early‑day flights avoid the premium associated with prime business‑hour departures. Checking multiple departure times on this page will help you pinpoint the cheapest slot.

Which airports serve Delhi and Dubai, and how far are they from the city centre?

Delhi is served by Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), which has three terminals and lies about 16 km (10 miles) west of New Delhi’s central business district, reachable by metro, taxi or bus. Dubai is served by Dubai International Airport (DXB), also with three terminals, situated roughly 15 km (9 miles) southeast of Dubai’s downtown, with metro, taxi and shuttle services linking the city centre.

How many flights operate between Delhi and Dubai, and what is the distance?

Around 20‑25 flights operate daily between Delhi and Dubai, covering a great‑circle distance of approximately 2,183 kilometres. This high frequency reflects strong business and tourism demand, with carriers offering multiple departures from early morning through late evening on both sides of the route.

What is the best time of year to visit Dubai, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Dubai is between November and March, when daytime temperatures average 24‑30 °C (75‑86 °F) and humidity is lower, making outdoor activities comfortable. Summer months (June‑August) see temperatures above 40 °C (104 °F) with high humidity, while the brief winter (December‑January) offers the coolest, most pleasant weather for sightseeing and desert excursions.

Which is the cheapest airline on the Delhi‑Dubai route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo is generally the cheapest airline on the Delhi‑Dubai route, offering base fares that are 20‑30 % lower than full‑service carriers. The trade‑off is a no‑frills experience: meals, free checked baggage and seat selection are not included, and the aircraft may have a tighter seat pitch. For travellers prioritising cost over amenities, IndiGo provides the most economical option.

Do I need a visa to travel from Delhi to Dubai, and how can I obtain one?

Indian citizens travelling from Delhi to Dubai require a visa, which can be obtained as an e‑visa through the UAE’s official portal or via a travel agency. The e‑visa process typically takes 3‑5 working days and allows a stay of up to 30 days for tourism. Ensure your passport is valid for at least six months and that you apply well before your intended departure date.
