Overview of the Delhi‑Mumbai air link

The Delhi to Mumbai corridor is one of India’s busiest domestic routes, linking the political capital with the financial hub. Flights are short, typically under two hours, and operate from early morning until late evening, giving travellers flexibility for business or leisure. The market is highly competitive, with multiple carriers offering frequent services, which keeps fares relatively affordable compared with many international routes. Because both airports are well‑connected to city centres, the overall door‑to‑door journey time is often less than three hours.

Getting to and from Indira Gandhi International Airport

Delhi’s Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) sits about 16 km west of the city centre. The Delhi Metro’s Airport Express Line links Terminal 3 directly to New Delhi railway station in roughly 20 minutes, and a fleet of taxis, app‑based rides and airport buses serve the other terminals. For those staying in South Delhi, the Delhi‑Gurgaon Expressway provides a fast road route, though traffic can be heavy during peak hours. The airport’s three terminals are clearly signposted, and most domestic airlines operate from Terminal 2 and Terminal 3.

Arriving at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport

Mumbai’s Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) lies about 8 km north of the city’s business district. Terminal 2 handles the majority of domestic flights, while Terminal 1 is used by low‑cost carriers. The Mumbai Suburban Railway’s Harbour Line reaches the airport via the nearby Vile Parle station, and a dedicated airport bus service connects to major hotels and the city centre. Taxis and app‑based rides are plentiful; the journey from the airport to South Mumbai usually takes 30‑45 minutes outside rush hour.

Finding the cheapest fares

Start by checking aggregate flight search engines early in the week, as many airlines release fare sales on Tuesdays. Set up price alerts for the Delhi‑Mumbai sector and compare the low‑cost carriers IndiGo, SpiceJet and GoFirst against full‑service airlines such as Air India and Vistara. Booking a few weeks ahead of a weekday departure, especially early morning or late evening, often yields the best rates. Consider flexible dates and be aware that peak business periods – early January and late November – can push prices higher.

Best time of year to travel and what to expect

The most pleasant months for flying between Delhi and Mumbai are October to February, when Delhi’s winter is cool and Mumbai’s monsoon season has ended. During the monsoon (June to September) occasional delays are possible, though flights usually run on schedule. Summer (March to May) brings high temperatures in Delhi, making early‑morning departures more comfortable. Festivals such as Diwali and the Mumbai Film Festival attract extra travellers, so expect higher demand and fares during those weeks.

Getting around Mumbai and top highlights

Mumbai’s local train network is the fastest way to traverse the city, with stations at Bandra, Andheri and Dadar close to the airport. Auto‑rickshaws and app‑based cabs provide door‑to‑door service, while the city’s bus system covers most suburbs. Key attractions include the historic Gateway of India, the bustling Colaba Causeway market, and the art‑deco heritage of Marine Drive. For a taste of the city’s film industry, a guided tour of the Bollywood studio complex in Andheri is a popular option.

How long is the flight from Delhi to Mumbai?

The scheduled flight time between Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) and Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) is about 1 hour 45 minutes, covering 1,137 km. Actual block time can vary by a few minutes due to air‑traffic control, and airlines may schedule up to 2 hours for connections at hub airports such as Delhi's Terminal 3. Most airlines schedule the same duration for both outbound and return services, making it one of India's shortest domestic sectors.

Which airlines fly from Delhi to Mumbai?

Air India, IndiGo, Vistara, SpiceJet and GoFirst operate regular services between Delhi (DEL) and Mumbai (BOM). Air India and Vistara provide full‑service cabins with meals and entertainment, while IndiGo, SpiceJet and GoFirst focus on low‑cost, high‑frequency schedules that run every hour during peak periods. All carriers use Delhi's Terminal 3 and Mumbai's Terminal 1 for departures and arrivals.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Delhi to Mumbai?

Yes, there are numerous direct, non‑stop flights each day between Delhi and Mumbai, offered by all major carriers on the route. On an average day you can find at least 30 non‑stop services, with departures roughly every 30‑45 minutes from early morning until late evening, making it one of the busiest domestic corridors in India.

Price drivers include booking window, travel day, time of departure, and demand around Indian holidays such as Diwali, Holi and Christmas. Mid‑week departures, especially on Tuesday or Wednesday, and early‑morning flights before 08:00 local time usually have the lowest fares on the Delhi‑Mumbai corridor. Airlines tend to price‑differentiate to fill seats left by business travellers, so a 06:30 departure often costs 15‑20 % less than a mid‑day 13:00 flight. Which airports serve Delhi and Mumbai, and how far are they from the city centre?

Delhi is served by Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) in the suburb of Palam, about 16 km from central Delhi, while Mumbai uses Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) in Andheri, roughly 20 km from downtown. Both airports are connected to their city centres by metro, express trains and a range of taxi services; the Delhi Airport Express Line reaches Terminal 3 in 20 minutes and Mumbai's Airport Line reaches Terminal 2 in about 30 minutes.

How many flights operate between Delhi and Mumbai, and what is the distance?

Around 30‑35 flights operate daily between Delhi (DEL) and Mumbai (BOM), covering a great‑circle distance of approximately 1,137 km. The high frequency is supported by multiple airlines and a mix of short‑haul aircraft such as the Airbus A320 family and Boeing 737, which keep turnaround times low and enable departures roughly every half hour during peak periods.

What is the best time of year to visit Mumbai, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Mumbai is between November and February, when the city enjoys mild temperatures of 20‑30 °C, low humidity and minimal rainfall. These winter months coincide with major cultural events such as the Kala Ghoda Arts Festival and the Mumbai Marathon, and provide comfortable conditions for sightseeing at landmarks like the Gateway of India and Marine Drive.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

IndiGo is generally the cheapest airline on the Delhi‑Mumbai route, offering base fares that are often 20‑30 % lower than full‑service carriers. The trade‑off is a no‑frills product with paid meals, stricter cabin‑baggage limits and limited seat selection, whereas airlines such as Air India or Vistara include complimentary meals, larger baggage allowances and a more extensive in‑flight entertainment offering.
