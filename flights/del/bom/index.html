Travelling from Delhi to Mumbai: what you need to know

  • Airports: departure from Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) – Terminal 3, 32 km north of Delhi city centre; arrival at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) – Terminal 1/2, 30 km south of Mumbai city centre.
  • Average direct flight time: 2 h 20 min (140 min).
  • Both cities are in the same time zone, IST (UTC+5:30); no time difference.
  • The most frequent pairing is DEL‑BOM, with over 300 non‑stop departures each week.
  • Arrive at the airport at least 2 hours before departure to clear security, immigration and boarding, especially during peak travel periods such as festivals and holidays.

How to find the cheapest flights from Delhi to Mumbai

  • Book 4–6 weeks in advance for the best fares; prices tend to rise as the travel date approaches.
  • The cheapest month to fly is usually January or February, when demand is lower after the New Year and the monsoon season has ended.
  • Set a price alert on a comparison site to be notified when fares dip below your target.
  • Use the “cheapest” or “fastest” filter to compare a range of airlines and identify the most cost‑effective option.

Flying from Delhi: Indira Gandhi International Airport

  • Airlines operating non‑stop services: Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, Akasa Air.
  • Common one‑stop hubs for alternative routes include Bangalore (BLR), Hyderabad (HYD) and Ahmedabad (AMD).
  • The airport is 32 km from Delhi city centre; car travel takes 45 min–1 h depending on traffic.
  • Delhi Metro’s Airport Express (Red Line) links the airport to New Delhi station in about 30 min, with a 5‑minute walk to the terminal.

Arriving in Mumbai: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport

  • The airport lies roughly 30 km south of Mumbai’s central business district.
  • By car the journey to the city centre takes 45 min–1 h; the Airport Link metro runs from the terminal to CST in 25 min.
  • Local trains from CST connect to suburbs such as Bandra, Andheri and Borivali; taxis and app‑based rides are also readily available.
  • Once in Mumbai, the local train network and the recently extended metro are the most efficient ways to reach popular districts.

Best time to go to Mumbai

  • The peak tourist season is November–December, when the weather is cooler and festivals such as Diwali attract visitors.
  • A cheaper and quieter period is January–February, with lower accommodation and flight prices.
  • Typical summer temperatures range from 25 °C to 30 °C; winter temperatures are 18 °C to 24 °C.
  • Monsoon season (June–September) brings heavy rain and high humidity, which may affect travel plans.

More about Mumbai

  • Iconic landmarks: Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus, Elephanta Caves.
  • Vibrant neighbourhoods: Bandra (hip cafes and boutiques), Juhu (beach and celebrity residences), Colaba (shopping and historic sites).
  • Cultural highlights include the Kala Ghoda Arts Festival in February and the Mumbai Film Festival in October.
  • The city’s culinary scene boasts street food stalls, fine dining restaurants and a wide range of regional Indian cuisine.

Explore more of India

  • Delhi – 1,137 km north of Mumbai; 2 h flight; home to the Red Fort, Humayun’s Tomb and India Gate.
  • Pune – 150 km south of Mumbai; 30 min train; known for its universities, Aga Khan Palace and Shaniwar Wada.
  • Hyderabad – 940 km east of Mumbai; 2 h flight; famous for Charminar, Golconda Fort and biryani.

Finding cheap flights from Delhi to Mumbai: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Delhi to Mumbai?

To locate inexpensive fares from Delhi (DEL) to Mumbai (BOM), search for flights on this page using flexible dates, as prices drop in January, February, March, September and October. Book 1–3 months ahead and avoid peak festival periods. Low‑cost carriers such as IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet usually offer the lowest prices. Set up price alerts on this page and compare the four airlines that operate the 43 daily flights to find the best deal.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Delhi to Mumbai?

The cheapest option is to fly with a low‑cost carrier—IndiGo, Akasa Air or SpiceJet—booking 6–8 weeks in advance and choosing a mid‑week departure. Avoid weekends and major holidays, and stay flexible with travel dates. These airlines offer fares that can be as low as a few hundred rupees, though they provide basic services and limited legroom compared with full‑service Air India.

When is the best time to book a flight from Delhi to Mumbai to get the lowest price?

The optimal booking window is 6–8 weeks before departure. Prices tend to rise as the travel date approaches, especially during school holidays and Indian festivals. Mid‑week flights are usually cheaper, and booking during the off‑peak season (late winter to early autumn) can yield further savings. Use the price‑alert feature on this page to monitor fare changes and book when a low‑price window opens.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Delhi to Mumbai?

January, February, March, September and October are the most economical months for Delhi‑to‑Mumbai flights. During these periods the demand is lower, so airlines offer discounted fares. Avoid the peak summer months of April to June and the monsoon season from July to September, when prices rise due to higher demand and weather‑related disruptions.

How long is the flight from Delhi to Mumbai?

The typical flight time from Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) is about 140 minutes, or roughly 2 hours and 20 minutes. The straight‑line distance between the two cities is 1,137 kilometres, and the flight is usually non‑stop.

Which airlines fly from Delhi to Mumbai?

Four airlines operate the Delhi‑to‑Mumbai route: Air India, IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet. Air India is the most frequent carrier, providing the highest number of daily flights. IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet are low‑cost operators that offer competitive fares and a high frequency of departures throughout the day.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Delhi to Mumbai?

Yes, all four carriers—Air India, IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet—provide direct, non‑stop services between Delhi (DEL) and Mumbai (BOM). Each flight covers the 1,137‑kilometre distance in about 2 hours and 20 minutes, and the route is served by 43 flights per day.

How much does a flight from Delhi to Mumbai usually cost and what drives the price?

Fares on the Delhi‑to‑Mumbai route typically range from INR 2,000 to INR 8,000, depending on the airline, booking window and season. Low‑cost carriers such as IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet offer the lowest prices, while Air India charges a premium for extra services. Prices increase during peak holiday periods, festivals and when booking last minute. Distance, seat class and airline reputation are the main factors influencing cost.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Mid‑week departures—Tuesday or Wednesday—are usually the cheapest, particularly if you book for mid‑morning (10:00–12:00) or early afternoon (13:00–15:00) slots. Weekend flights tend to be more expensive, and early‑morning or late‑night flights can also carry a premium. Checking fares on this page for these time windows often reveals the lowest rates.

Which airports serve Delhi and Mumbai, and how far are they from the city centre?

Delhi is served by Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), located about 16 kilometres from the city centre and featuring three terminals. Mumbai is served by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM), situated roughly 30 kilometres from the city centre and comprising two terminals. Both airports are well connected to downtown via metro, local trains, taxis and app‑based cabs.

How many flights operate between Delhi and Mumbai, and what is the distance?

The Delhi‑to‑Mumbai corridor sees 43 flights per day, operated by Air India, IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet. The straight‑line distance between the two cities is 1,137 kilometres, and the typical flight duration is 140 minutes.

What is the best time of year to visit Mumbai and what is the weather like?

The most pleasant period to visit Mumbai is from November to February, when temperatures range from 20 °C to 25 °C and humidity is moderate. April to June can be hot, with temperatures reaching 30 °C to 35 °C, while July to September brings heavy monsoon rainfall and high humidity. The winter months offer comfortable weather for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route and what is the trade‑off?

Akasa Air and IndiGo generally offer the lowest fares on the Delhi‑to‑Mumbai route. The trade‑off is that these low‑cost carriers provide basic in‑flight services, limited legroom and no complimentary meals, whereas Air India offers more spacious seating, onboard meals and a higher level of customer service at a higher price.

What are the best transport options from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport to Mumbai city centre?

From BOM, you can reach Mumbai city centre via the Metro Line 1, which takes about 45 minutes to reach CST and costs a few rupees. Local trains on the Harbour Line also connect the airport to central stations like Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus. Taxis, app‑based cabs and private car hires are available; fares range from INR 400 to 600 for a 30‑kilometre journey and travel time is 45–60 minutes depending on traffic.
