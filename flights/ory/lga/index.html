Travelling from Paris to New York: what you need to know

  • Depart from Paris Orly Airport (ORY) and arrive at LaGuardia Airport (LGA). - The typical non‑stop flight time between the two cities is about 8 hours, although there are currently no scheduled direct services on this pairing. - Paris operates on Central European Time (UTC+1, summer UTC+2) while New York uses Eastern Standard Time (UTC‑5, summer UTC‑4), giving a time‑difference of six hours for most of the year. - The busiest airport‑to‑airport traffic between the capitals runs between Paris Charles‑de‑Gaulle (CDG) and New York JFK, not ORY‑LGA. - For an international departure, aim to be at the airport at least two hours before the scheduled check‑in time, and allow extra time during summer holidays.

How to find the cheapest flights from Paris to New York

  • Book 8‑12 weeks ahead for the best balance of price and choice; ultra‑early bookings (six months+) can sometimes be cheaper for peak‑summer travel. - January and February are historically the cheapest months, with fare drops of 15‑25 % compared with June‑August. - Set up price‑alert notifications on comparison sites and filter results by “cheapest” or “shortest” to spot sudden drops. - When using the filters, also tick the box for “include nearby airports” to compare ORY‑LGA with CDG‑JFK or Newark, which often yields lower fares.

Flying from Paris: Paris Orly Airport

  • Main carriers that operate non‑stop services from ORY to the United States include Air France (to JFK) and Delta Air Lines (seasonal to LGA). - Common one‑stop connections for ORY‑LGA travellers are via London Heathrow (LHR), Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS), Frankfurt (FRA) or Doha (DOH). - Orly lies roughly 13 km south‑south‑west of Paris centre; the drive takes about 30 minutes by taxi, while the RER B train from Antony station reaches the airport in around 30 minutes with a short shuttle bus ride.

Arriving in New York: LaGuardia Airport

  • LaGuardia sits about 13 km east of Manhattan; a taxi or rideshare typically needs 20‑30 minutes, depending on traffic. - Public transport options include the M60 SBS bus to the Astoria subway station, where you can connect to the N/W lines, or the Q70 SBS to the 7 train at Jackson Heights. - Once in Manhattan, the extensive subway and commuter‑rail network provides quick access to all boroughs; a MetroCard works on buses, subways and the AirTrain to nearby JFK if you need to transfer.

Best time to go to New York

  • The most popular month for visitors is June, when the city hosts many festivals and the weather is warm. - For a quieter, cheaper experience, aim for January or early February, when hotel rates fall and crowds thin. - Summer temperatures average 25‑30 °C, while winter averages hover around 0‑5 °C, with occasional snow in January.

More about New York

  • Iconic sites include Times Square, Central Park, the Statue of Liberty and the Metropolitan Museum of Art. - Neighborhoods such as Williamsburg in Brooklyn, the West Village and Harlem each offer distinct cultural flavours, dining scenes and historic architecture. - The city’s five boroughs provide endless variety, from the skyscrapers of Midtown to the waterfront parks of Queens.

Explore more of United States

  • Boston, Massachusetts – about 340 km north of New York; attractions include the Freedom Trail and Harvard University. - Washington, D.C. – roughly 360 km south; highlights are the National Mall, Smithsonian museums and the Capitol. - Philadelphia, Pennsylvania – only 150 km southwest; famous for Independence Hall, the Liberty Bell and a vibrant arts district.

Finding cheap flights from Paris to New York: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Paris to New York?

To find cheap flights from Paris Orly (ORY) to LaGuardia (LGA) you should search with flexible dates, compare the whole month, and set price alerts on this page. The lowest fares normally appear when travelling in January, February or early November and when you book 6‑8 weeks in advance. Checking nearby airports – Paris Charles‑de‑Gaulle (CDG) and New York JFK (JFK) or Newark (EWR) – often uncovers cheaper connections, especially with mixed‑carrier itineraries.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Paris to New York?

The cheapest way to fly from Paris Orly (ORY) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) is to combine a low‑cost carrier from ORY to a European hub and then a budget transatlantic airline, or to use a single carrier's basic economy fare on a non‑stop service. Airlines such as Norwegian (seasonal) or LEVEL often publish sub‑€400 round‑trip fares, while Air France and Delta’s basic economy can be cheaper when booked early and combined with a short domestic hop to LGA.

When is the best time to book a flight from Paris to New York to get the lowest price?

The best time to book a flight from Paris Orly (ORY) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) for the lowest price is typically 6‑8 weeks before departure, with an additional dip in fares about 3‑4 months ahead of the summer peak. Monitoring fare calendars on this page and setting alerts can capture flash sales that usually appear on Tuesdays. Avoiding booking around French school holidays in July‑August and US Thanksgiving in late November also helps keep the cost down.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Paris to New York?

The cheapest month to fly from Paris Orly (ORY) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) is usually January, followed by February and early November, when demand is lowest and airlines run clearance sales. Late autumn and early spring also produce lower fares compared with the summer travel rush and the December holiday period. Booking for mid‑week departures during these months typically yields the best price, while avoiding French school vacations and US Thanksgiving maximises savings.

How long is the flight from Paris to New York?

The flight distance between Paris Orly (ORY) and New York LaGuardia (LGA) is approximately 5,831 kilometres, and a non‑stop service usually lasts around 7 hours and 45 minutes in the air. Tailwinds can shorten the east‑to‑west leg to just under 7 hours, while headwinds may extend it to about 8½ hours. Most airlines schedule the flight for a morning departure from Paris, arriving in New York early afternoon local time.

Which airlines fly from Paris to New York?

Airlines that operate flights between Paris Orly (ORY) and New York LaGuardia (LGA) include Air France, Delta Air Lines, American Airlines, United Airlines and seasonal low‑cost carrier Norwegian Air Shuttle. Air France and Delta provide the most frequent non‑stop services, while American and United often route through their US hubs before landing at LGA. Norwegian runs a few weekly flights during the winter season, offering a basic‑economy product at lower fares.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Paris to New York?

There are direct, non‑stop flights from Paris Orly (ORY) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) operated by Air France and Delta Air Lines, though the schedule is limited compared with the busier Paris‑Charles de Gaulle to JFK routes. These non‑stop services typically run once or twice daily in each direction, mainly in the morning from Paris and early afternoon from New York. For more frequent options travellers can also consider connecting flights via JFK or Newark (EWR).

How much does a flight from Paris to New York usually cost and what drives the price?

A typical economy fare from Paris Orly (ORY) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) ranges between €300 and €800, with lower ends appearing in January, February or early November and higher prices during summer, Christmas and US Thanksgiving. Price drivers include the booking window, day‑of‑week demand, fuel costs, airline competition and whether the ticket is basic‑economy or includes flexible conditions. Using price alerts on this page and travelling mid‑week can help you capture the €300‑€400 band.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

The cheapest day to fly from Paris Orly (ORY) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) is usually Tuesday or Wednesday, and flights departing early in the morning (around 06:00‑08:00 local time) or late evening (after 20:00) tend to have the lowest fares. Mid‑week departures avoid the business‑travel surge on Monday and Thursday, while off‑peak departure times reduce demand on the aircraft, prompting airlines to discount seats. Checking the fare calendar on this page confirms the trend for each month.

Which airports serve New York and Paris, and how far are they from the city centre?

Paris is served by Paris Orly Airport (ORY), located about 13 km south‑south‑west of the city centre, while New York’s LaGuardia Airport (LGA) sits roughly 8 km east of Midtown Manhattan. Both airports have a single terminal building, and each offers express bus, train and rideshare connections to central districts. Alternative airports include Paris Charles‑de‑Gaulle (CDG) 25 km north of Paris and New York’s JFK (≈24 km) or Newark Liberty (≈16 km) for more flight options.

How many flights operate between Paris and New York, and what is the distance?

Approximately 12‑14 scheduled flights per day operate between Paris and New York across all airports, covering a great‑circle distance of about 5,831 km. This includes non‑stop services from Paris Orly (ORY) to LaGuardia (LGA) as well as connections via Charles‑de‑Gaulle to JFK or Newark (EWR). Frequency rises to 18‑20 flights on peak travel days in summer, while winter weekdays may see only eight departures combined.

What is the best time of year to visit New York, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit New York is during late spring (April‑June) or early autumn (September‑October) when temperatures are mild, humidity is moderate and the city hosts numerous outdoor festivals. Average highs range from 15 °C in April to 24 °C in May and 22 °C in October, with occasional rain. Summer can be hot and humid, while winter brings snow and sub‑zero temperatures, making spring and autumn the most comfortable periods for sightseeing.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

The cheapest airline on the Paris Orly (ORY) to New York LaGuardia (LGA) route is usually Norwegian Air Shuttle, which offers basic‑economy fares that can fall below €350 during off‑peak periods. The trade‑off is a reduced service level: no complimentary meals, limited checked baggage allowance and fewer flight‑change options compared with full‑service carriers such as Air France or Delta. Passengers should also consider that Norwegian operates only a few weekly flights, mainly in the winter season.

What is the easiest way to travel from LaGuardia Airport to Manhattan after arriving from Paris?

The easiest way to travel from LaGuardia Airport (LGA) to Manhattan after a flight from Paris Orly (ORY) is to take the M60 Select Bus Service to the Astoria‑Ditmars Boulevard subway station, then transfer to the N or W line into Midtown. Alternatively, a direct taxi or rideshare costs around €70‑€90 and takes 20‑30 minutes without traffic. Airport shuttle services also operate between LGA and major Manhattan hotels, offering a convenient door‑to‑door option.
