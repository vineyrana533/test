Overview of the Newark to Puerto Vallarta flight

The route covers roughly 3,680 kilometres and is served primarily by United Airlines, American Airlines and Aeromexico. Direct services are usually scheduled for the late afternoon or early evening, giving travellers a convenient arrival time in the early morning of the next day. The flight takes approximately 7 hours and 30 minutes, a bit longer than the great circle calculation due to air‑traffic routing and a slight detour over the Caribbean. For those who prefer a stopover, a connection via Mexico City adds about 2 to 3 hours of layover time but can sometimes offer lower fares.

Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR)

EWR sits in the suburbs of Newark, New Jersey, and is one of the busiest airports in the United States. The airport has three terminals (A, B and C) and is served by a range of public transport links. The Airport Express train connects the airport to Newark Penn Station in just 15 minutes, and from there a short train ride takes you into Manhattan or to New York’s Penn Station. Buses, taxis and rideshares are also readily available, with a typical journey to downtown Manhattan taking around 45 minutes depending on traffic.

Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR)

PVR is a single‑terminal airport located about 8 kilometres south of Puerto Vallarta’s historic centre. It handles a mix of domestic and international traffic and is equipped with modern facilities including a duty‑free shop, a café and free Wi‑Fi. From the airport the most common transport options are taxis, rideshare services and the local bus network. A taxi ride to the city centre takes around 15 minutes, while the local bus takes roughly 30 minutes and offers a cheaper, albeit slower, alternative.

Finding the cheapest fares on this route

The most reliable way to spot a good deal is to compare prices across the three main carriers – United, American and Aeromexico – and to use fare‑comparison sites that allow you to filter by departure time and layover length. Booking 6–8 weeks in advance typically yields the lowest fares, especially for mid‑week departures. Signing up for fare alerts or using the airlines’ own price‑watch tools can also help you snap up a discount when one appears. Avoiding peak travel periods such as Christmas and the summer holidays is another key strategy for keeping costs down.

Best time of year to travel and what to expect

Puerto Vallarta enjoys a tropical climate with warm temperatures year‑round. The dry season from December to April offers sunny skies and lower humidity, making it ideal for beach activities and sightseeing. Prices are usually at their lowest in January and February, and mid‑week flights are often cheaper than weekend departures. If you prefer fewer crowds and can tolerate higher humidity, the wet season from May to November still offers good value, but be prepared for occasional showers.

Getting around and highlights in Puerto Vallarta

Once in Puerto Vallarta, the historic Zona Romántica and the Malecón boardwalk are best explored on foot or via the free local bus system. For a more relaxed experience, taxis and rideshare apps are plentiful and inexpensive. Key attractions include the Church of Our Lady of Guadalupe, Los Arcos (the iconic sea arch), the art‑filled streets of the Old Town, and a day trip to the nearby Marietas Islands for snorkeling and beach‑combing. The city’s culinary scene is renowned for fresh seafood and traditional Mexican dishes, and the local markets offer a colourful glimpse into regional culture.

What is the best time of year to visit Puerto Vallarta, and what is the weather like?

Puerto Vallarta is best visited from March to May and September to November. During these shoulder seasons the weather is warm, with average highs around 28 °C (82 °F) and lower humidity. Rainfall is minimal, and the sea is comfortable for swimming. Summer (June–August) can be hot and humid, while December–January sees cooler temperatures and occasional showers.
