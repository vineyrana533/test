Travelling from Newark to Edmonton: what you need to know

  • Depart from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) in New Jersey; arrive at Edmonton International Airport (YEG) in Alberta.
  • The average non‑stop flight covers 3 248 km in roughly four hours.
  • Edmonton operates on Mountain Time (UTC‑7/‑6) – two hours behind Newark’s Eastern Time (UTC‑5/‑4).
  • The busiest airport‑to‑airport pairing on this corridor is EWR ↔ YEG.
  • For international departures, aim to be at the terminal at least two hours before take‑off, allowing extra time during summer holidays.

How to find the cheapest flights from Newark to Edmonton

  • Book 6–8 weeks ahead for the best balance of price and seat availability.
  • February is consistently the cheapest month, with January close behind.
  • Set up a price‑alert on a comparison site to be notified of drops.
  • Use the “price” or “duration” filter to sort results and spot the lowest‑cost or fastest itineraries.

Flying from Newark: Newark Liberty International Airport

  • United Airlines and Air Canada operate the only scheduled non‑stop services, mainly in the winter schedule.
  • Common one‑stop hubs are Toronto Pearson (YYZ), Vancouver International (YVR) and Calgary International (YYC).
  • The airport lies about 24 km from downtown Manhattan; a car journey takes around 30 minutes, while the AirTrain + NJ Transit route is roughly 45 minutes to the city centre.

Arriving in Edmonton: Edmonton International Airport

  • YEG sits roughly 27 km south of Edmonton’s central business district; the drive to downtown is about 30 minutes.
  • Public transport includes the Edmonton Transit Service Route 747 shuttle (≈45 minutes) and regular city buses.
  • Taxis, rideshare services and on‑site car‑rental desks are available for onward travel.

Best time to go to Edmonton

  • July is the most popular month, driven by the Edmonton International Street Performers Festival and warm weather.
  • February offers lower accommodation rates and fewer crowds, ideal for winter sport enthusiasts.
  • Summer temperatures average 15‑25 °C, while winter lows range from –15 °C to –5 °C, with occasional snowfall.

More about Edmonton

  • West Edmonton Mall – one of the world’s largest shopping complexes, featuring an indoor water park and amusement rides.
  • Fort Edmonton Park – a living history museum that recreates the city’s development from the 1840s to the 1920s.
  • River Valley – a 7 500‑hectare network of parks and trails perfect for cycling and walking.
  • Royal Alberta Museum – showcases Indigenous culture, natural history and provincial heritage.

Explore more of Alberta

  • Calgary (≈300 km south) – highlights include the Calgary Tower, the Glenbow Museum and the annual Calgary Stampede.
  • Banff (≈400 km southwest) – gateway to Banff National Park, Lake Louise and the Canadian Rockies.
  • Jasper (≈350 km northwest) – known for Jasper National Park, the Maligne Canyon and dark‑sky stargazing.

Finding cheap flights from Newark to Edmonton: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Newark to Edmonton?

The cheapest Newark‑to‑Edmonton flights are found by keeping travel dates flexible, searching a range of +/- three days, and targeting the off‑peak winter months. Booking 6‑8 weeks ahead usually yields the best fares, and setting a price alert on this page will notify you of drops. Consider nearby U.S. airports such as JFK or LGA for departures and Canadian gateways like Calgary (YYC) for arrivals, as connecting itineraries often cost less than a direct run.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Newark to Edmonton?

The most economical way to travel from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Edmonton International Airport (YEG) is to combine a low‑cost carrier on the Canadian leg with a major U.S. airline on the trans‑border segment, typically routing via a hub such as Calgary or Vancouver. Booking a mixed‑carrier itinerary, travelling on a Tuesday or Wednesday, and avoiding peak holiday periods can reduce the round‑trip cost to the lower end of the typical price range.

When is the best time to book a flight from Newark to Edmonton to get the lowest price?

The optimal booking window for Newark‑to‑Edmonton flights is 6‑8 weeks before departure, when airlines release discounted seats but before the final fare rise close to the travel date. Monitoring fares in January and February for spring travel, or in September for autumn trips, often yields the lowest prices. Setting a price alert on this page and checking weekly can capture sudden promotions that appear during this window.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Newark to Edmonton?

January, February and early March are generally the cheapest months to fly from Newark (EWR) to Edmonton (YEG), with average round‑trip fares 15‑25 % lower than summer rates. Late October also offers reduced prices as demand falls after the summer peak. These periods coincide with Edmonton’s colder season, when leisure travel demand is lower, making airlines more likely to discount seats.

How long is the flight from Newark to Edmonton?

A non‑stop flight between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Edmonton International Airport (YEG) takes roughly 5 hours 30 minutes, covering 3,248 km. Most itineraries include one stop in a U.S. or Canadian hub, extending total travel time to 7‑10 hours depending on layover length. Flight time can vary slightly with wind conditions, but the typical airborne segment remains just under six hours.

Which airlines fly from Newark to Edmonton?

United Airlines operates the primary service between Newark (EWR) and Edmonton (YEG), often with a connection in Chicago or Denver. Air Canada and WestJet provide one‑stop options via Calgary (YYC) or Vancouver (YVR). Delta Air Lines and American Airlines also serve the route with connections through their U.S. hubs such as Atlanta (ATL) or Dallas (DFW). All carriers use modern narrow‑body aircraft on the trans‑border segment.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Newark to Edmonton?

United Airlines runs seasonal non‑stop flights between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Edmonton International Airport (YEG) during the winter and early spring demand window. Outside of that period, the route is served only by one‑stop itineraries, typically connecting through a U.S. hub like Chicago O’Hare (ORD) or a Canadian hub such as Calgary (YYC). Checking the current schedule on this page will confirm whether a direct service is available for your travel dates.

How much does a flight from Newark to Edmonton usually cost and what drives the price?

Round‑trip fares between Newark (EWR) and Edmonton (YEG) normally range from US$300 to US$700, with the lower end appearing in winter and the higher end in summer. Prices are driven by seasonal demand, fuel price fluctuations, the number of available seats on a given carrier, and how far in advance the ticket is purchased. Additional factors include whether the itinerary is direct, the chosen airline’s fare class, and any ancillary services such as checked baggage.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Travelling on Tuesdays or Wednesdays generally produces the lowest fares on the Newark‑to‑Edmonton corridor, as business‑travel demand is weaker mid‑week. Early‑morning departures before 09:00 UTC or late‑evening flights after 20:00 UTC tend to be cheaper than midday services, because they fill less‑popular slots. Combining a mid‑week travel day with an off‑peak time of day can shave up to 20 % off the typical fare range.

Which airports serve Edmonton and Newark, and how far are they from the city centre?

Edmonton International Airport (YEG) lies about 30 km west of downtown Edmonton and is reached by a 30‑minute drive via Highway 2 or the dedicated Airport Express bus. Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) is situated roughly 16 km south of Manhattan, New York City, and is accessible by the AirTrain to NJ Transit and Amtrak services, taking about 30 minutes to reach Penn Station. Both airports have single‑terminal layouts for passenger processing.

How many flights operate between Newark and Edmonton, and what is the distance?

On average, two to three scheduled flights per day operate between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Edmonton International Airport (YEG), counting both direct and one‑stop services. The great‑circle distance between the two airports is approximately 3,248 km (2,018 miles), which translates to about a five‑hour‑plus airborne segment on a non‑stop flight.

What is the best time of year to visit Edmonton, and what is the weather like?

The most popular time to visit Edmonton is June through August, when daytime temperatures average 20‑25 °C and the city hosts events such as the Edmonton International Fringe Festival. Early September offers mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter months are cold, with average lows around –15 °C and frequent snow, appealing to visitors of the Ice Castles and winter sports. Spring can be variable, with rapid temperature swings.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

WestJet often provides the lowest base fare on Newark‑to‑Edmonton itineraries, especially when the Canadian leg departs from Calgary (YYC) or Vancouver (YVR). The trade‑off is that WestJet’s lowest‑price tickets may have stricter change‑fee policies, limited seat selection, and fewer complimentary services compared with United’s standard economy fares. Passengers prioritising cost over flexibility typically choose WestJet, while those seeking more amenities may prefer United.

What major Canadian hub can I use to connect from Newark to Edmonton?

Calgary International Airport (YYC) is the most common Canadian hub for connecting flights from Newark to Edmonton. United Airlines and Air Canada operate frequent Newark‑to‑Calgary services, after which WestJet or Air Canada provide short domestic flights of about one hour to Edmonton. Using Calgary as a transfer point often yields lower overall fares and more flight options, especially when travelling outside the peak summer season.
