Travelling from Newark to Saskatoon: what you need to know

  • Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) is the main departure point, with three terminals.
  • Saskatoon John G. Diefenbaker International Airport (YXE) is the only major airport in Saskatoon.
  • Direct flights are currently not available; most itineraries include a stopover in Toronto (YYZ), Calgary (YYC) or Edmonton (YEG).
  • A non‑stop flight, if offered, would take roughly 4 h 30 min.
  • The time‑zone difference is one hour: Saskatoon is UTC‑6 (UTC‑5 in summer) while Newark is UTC‑5 (UTC‑4 in summer).
  • The most frequent route uses Newark → Toronto → Saskatoon, operated by Air Canada and United.
  • Arrive at EWR at least 2 h before an international departure, and 3 h if you have checked baggage during peak season.

How to find the cheapest flights from Newark to Saskatoon

  • Book 2–3 months in advance for the best fares; prices rise sharply in June–August.
  • The cheapest month to fly is usually January or February, when demand is low.
  • Set a price alert on a comparison site and monitor for a drop of 10–15 %.
  • Use the filter to sort by “cheapest” then “shortest duration” to see the most economical itineraries.
  • Avoid the weekend peak; mid‑week flights (Tuesday–Thursday) tend to be cheaper.

Flying from Newark: Newark Liberty International Airport

  • Air Canada, United, Delta and WestJet all operate non‑stop to Toronto, Calgary or Edmonton; these are the main hubs for connecting to Saskatoon.
  • The airport is about 20 km west of downtown Newark, roughly a 30‑minute drive.
  • The AirTrain to Newark Penn Station takes 15 min, then a 10‑minute walk to the train platform for the NJ Transit service to New York or the Amtrak route to Canada.
  • Buses (NJT Bus 62) run every 30 min to the station and to downtown Newark.

Arriving in Saskatoon: Saskatoon John G. Diefenbaker International Airport

  • YXE lies about 25 km east of downtown Saskatoon, a 30‑minute drive.
  • The airport is served by the Saskatoon Transit bus route 1, which takes roughly 45 min to the city centre; a taxi takes 20 min.
  • From the terminal, the “Saskatoon Airport Shuttle” offers a free ride to the downtown bus stop.
  • Once in the city, local buses and taxis are plentiful; a short walk or bike ride can take you to the main shopping and cultural districts.

Best time to go to Saskatoon

  • The most popular month is July, when festivals and outdoor activities peak.
  • A cheaper and quieter period is late September to early October, just before the fall foliage.
  • Summer temperatures average 18–25 °C, while winter averages –10 to –20 °C; the city experiences long, cold winters with snow.
  • Spring (April–May) offers milder weather (10–15 °C) and fewer crowds.

More about Saskatoon

  • The historic Nutana neighbourhood boasts Victorian homes and the Canadian Museum of History.
  • The Meewasin Valley Trail offers scenic walks along the South Saskatchewan River.
  • The Saskatoon Farmers' Market, open year‑round, showcases local produce and crafts.
  • The downtown area is home to the Tower of London‑style City Hall and the lively Broadway Avenue.

Explore more of Saskatchewan

  • Regina – 260 km south – is the provincial capital; key sights include the Saskatchewan Legislative Building and the Royal Saskatchewan Museum.
  • Prince Albert – 350 km north – offers the Prince Albert National Park with its dramatic boreal forest.
  • Moose Jaw – 350 km west – is famous for its underground tunnels and the Tunnels of Moose Jaw.
