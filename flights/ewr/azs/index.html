Travelling from Newark to Samaná: what you need to know

  • Depart from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), the only major airport serving Newark, with three terminals.
  • Arrive at Samaná El Catey International Airport (AZS), the sole commercial airport in the Samaná Peninsula.
  • A non‑stop flight would cover roughly 2 419 km in about 4 h 30 min; in practice you will connect, adding 2‑5 h of layover time.
  • Newark operates on UTC‑5 (Eastern Standard Time) while Samaná is on UTC‑4 (Atlantic Standard Time), a one‑hour forward shift.
  • The busiest airport‑to‑airport pairing on this route is Newark – Punta Cana (PUJ), followed by a ground transfer to Samaná.
  • Arrive at least two hours before an international departure; allow extra time during summer holidays and winter holidays.

How to find the cheapest flights from Newark to Samaná

  • Book 8‑12 weeks ahead for the best mix of price and availability; last‑minute deals are rare on this long‑haul route.
  • September, October and early November are consistently the cheapest months, avoiding the winter‑holiday surge.
  • Set up price‑alert tools on major comparison sites; they will email you when fares drop below your target.
  • Use the “cheapest” or “shortest‑duration” filter to compare basic‑economy fares versus premium options.
  • Consider flying into Punta Cana (PUJ) and taking a shuttle; the combined cost is often lower than a direct ticket to AZS.

Flying from Newark: Newark Liberty International Airport

  • United Airlines, American Airlines, Delta Air Lines and JetBlue Airways all operate itineraries from EWR to Samaná, usually with one stop.
  • Common one‑stop hubs are Punta Cana (PUJ), Santo Domingo (SDQ) and Miami (MIA).
  • EWR lies about 24 km (15 mi) from downtown Newark; driving takes 20‑30 minutes depending on traffic.
  • The AirTrain links all three terminals to NJ Transit and Amtrak; the total public‑transport journey to the city centre is roughly 45 minutes.
  • Parking garages and short‑term lots are available; a 24‑hour stay costs around US$30‑$40.

Arriving in Samaná: El Catey International Airport

  • AZS is situated about 10 km (6 mi) east of Samaná town; the drive to the centre takes 15‑20 minutes by car.
  • Taxis are the most common on‑airport option; a fixed‑rate ride to the town costs roughly US$25.
  • A shared shuttle service runs twice daily, taking about 30 minutes and dropping passengers at major hotels.
  • Public minibusses (guagua) connect the airport with nearby villages; the journey to central Samaná is around 35 minutes.
  • Once in town, rental cars, scooters and local taxis make it easy to explore the peninsula’s beaches and parks.

Best time to go to Samaná

  • July is the most popular month, coinciding with the hump‑back whale‑watching season.
  • For lower prices and fewer crowds, aim for September or early October.
  • Summer (June‑August) sees average highs of 29‑31 °C and lows of 24‑26 °C; winter (December‑February) remains warm at 26‑28 °C daytime, 22‑24 °C at night.
  • The rainy season peaks from May to November, with brief afternoon showers that rarely disrupt travel.

More about Samaná

  • Los Haitises National Park, a mangrove‑filled karst landscape accessible by boat from the town of Sabana de la Mar.
  • Playa Rincón, often listed among the Caribbean’s most beautiful beaches, offers powder‑white sand and clear turquoise water.
  • Whale‑watching tours depart from the Bay of Samaná between January and March, when hump‑back whales migrate to breed.
  • Cayo Levantado (Bacardi Island) is a short ferry ride away, popular for snorkeling and day‑trips.

Explore more of the Dominican Republic

  • Santo Domingo, the capital, lies about 200 km south of Samaná; its Colonial Zone is a UNESCO World Heritage site and the first European settlement in the New World.
  • Punta Cana, roughly 210 km east, is famous for its all‑inclusive resorts, the Indigenous Eyes Ecological Park and the nearby Hoyo Azul lagoon.
  • Puerto Plata, around 250 km north, offers historic Fort San Felipe, the Amber Museum and the scenic cable‑car ride up Mount Isabel de Barrios.

The cheapest month to fly from Newark to Samaná is generally January, February or early November, when demand is lowest and airlines run promotions. How long is the flight from Newark to Samaná?

The scheduled flight time between Newark (EWR) and Samaná (AZS) is about five hours on a non‑stop sector, but most itineraries include a stop, extending total travel time to roughly nine to twelve hours depending on layover length. Direct sectors are rare, so passengers should allow extra time for connections at hubs such as Miami (MIA) or Panama City (PTY).

Which airlines fly from Newark to Samaná?

Airlines that operate between Newark and Samaná include American Airlines, Delta Air Lines and United Airlines on codeshare arrangements, as well as Caribbean carriers such as JetBlue, Copa Airlines and Spirit Airlines. Which airports serve Samaná and Newark, and how far are they from the city centre?

Samaná El Catey International Airport (AZS) has a single terminal located roughly 10 km (6 miles) north of the town centre of Samaná, with taxis and shuttle services providing easy access to hotels and beaches.

How many flights operate between Newark and Samaná, and what is the distance?

Approximately eight to ten scheduled flights operate each week between Newark and Samaná, covering a great‑circle distance of 2 419 km (about 1 503 miles). The frequency varies by season, with additional services added during the winter tourism peak and fewer flights in the summer low‑season.

What is the best time of year to visit Samaná, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Samaná is from December to April, when the weather is dry, temperatures range from 24 °C to 30 °C and humidity is moderate. This period also coincides with the hump‑back whale‑watching season, which peaks between January and March, making it the most popular time for tourists seeking beach and wildlife experiences.

What is the most convenient connecting hub for flights from Newark to Samaná?

The most convenient connecting hub for flights from Newark to Samaná is Miami International Airport (MIA), because it provides frequent onward services to AZS and short ground times, making total journey time competitive. Miami’s extensive network of Caribbean flights and its proximity to the Dominican Republic reduce layover duration and simplify transfers.
