Travelling from Newark to Montreal: what you need to know

  • Depart from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), the city’s primary international hub with three terminals.
  • Arrive at Montréal‑Trudeau International Airport (YUL), the main gateway to the city with a single passenger terminal.
  • Average non‑stop flight time is about 1 hour 20 minutes, covering roughly 533 km.
  • Both airports operate on Eastern Time (UTC‑5 standard, UTC‑4 daylight), so there is no time‑zone change.
  • The busiest pairing on this route is EWR ↔ YUL, with multiple daily services.
  • Arrive at least two hours before departure for international checks; add an extra half hour during summer holidays.

How to find the cheapest flights from Newark to Montreal

  • Book 6‑8 weeks ahead for the best mix of price and availability; last‑minute fares tend to surge.
  • January and February are historically the cheapest months, with lower demand after the holiday period.
  • Set a price‑alert on a comparison site to be notified of drops; most alerts trigger within a few days of a fare change.
  • Use the “cheapest” filter first, then sort by “shortest duration” to balance cost and travel time.

Flying from Newark: Newark Liberty International Airport

  • Air Canada and United Airlines operate daily non‑stop flights; American and Delta provide one‑stop options via Toronto (YYZ) or Chicago (ORD).
  • Common connection hubs include Toronto Pearson (YYZ), Chicago O’Hare (ORD) and Detroit Metropolitan (DTW).
  • The airport lies about 16 km southwest of downtown Newark; a car journey takes 20‑25 minutes without traffic.
  • Public transport via NJ Transit’s Northeast Corridor Line reaches the airport in roughly 45 minutes from New York Penn Station.

Arriving in Montreal: Montréal‑Trudeau International Airport

  • YUL is situated 20 km east of downtown Montreal; the drive to the city centre usually takes 25‑30 minutes.
  • The 747 Express Bus links the terminal with Central Station in 45 minutes, running every 15‑20 minutes.
  • Within the city, the STM network (metro and bus) provides extensive coverage; a one‑way fare is CAD 3.50.
  • Taxis and rideshare services are readily available, with a typical downtown drop‑off costing CAD 30‑35.

Best time to go to Montreal

  • July and August are the most popular months, drawing tourists to festivals and outdoor cafés.
  • For lower prices and smaller crowds, travel in January or early February.
  • Summer temperatures average 20‑27 °C, while winter averages –5 °C to –10 °C, with occasional dips below –15 °C.
  • Spring (April‑May) and early autumn (September) offer mild weather and vibrant foliage, ideal for walking tours.

More about Montreal

  • Old Montreal (Vieux‑Montréal) features cobbled streets, the historic Notre‑Dame Basilica and the waterfront promenade.
  • Mount Royal Park provides panoramic city views and seasonal activities such as sledding in winter.
  • The Jean‑Talon Market showcases local produce, cheese and artisanal goods.
  • Montréal’s vibrant neighbourhoods – Plateau Mont‑Royal, Mile End and Little Italy – are renowned for cafés, boutiques and street art.

Explore more of Canada

  • Quebec City, about 250 km east of Montreal, is famous for its UNESCO‑listed Old Town and Château Frontenac.
  • Ottawa, roughly 200 km west, offers Parliament Hill, the Rideau Canal and national museums.
  • Toronto, around 540 km west, boasts the CN Tower, Royal Ontario Museum and a bustling waterfront.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Newark to Montreal?

Selecting flights that depart early in the morning or late at night often reduces the fare, and avoiding major holidays such as Thanksgiving and Christmas prevents price spikes. When is the best time to book a flight from Newark to Montreal to get the lowest price?

The best time to book a flight from Newark (EWR) to Montreal (YUL) for the lowest price is about 45‑60 days before departure, especially for travel in the low‑season months of January, February and early November. Monitoring fares on this page and setting a price alert can capture the occasional fare‑sale that airlines run in late summer. What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Newark to Montreal?

The cheapest month to fly from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Montreal‑Trudeau (YUL) is typically January, followed by February and early November, when demand is low and airlines offer their deepest discounts. During these months the average round‑trip fare in economy class ranges between US$150 and US$250, whereas summer travel in June‑August often costs double. Avoiding Canadian holidays such as Canada Day (1 July) also helps keep the price down.

How long is the flight from Newark to Montreal?

The flight time between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Montreal‑Trudeau International Airport (YUL) is about 1 hour and 30 minutes on a non‑stop service. The 533 km (331 mi) distance is covered by carriers such as United, Air Canada and JetBlue, with slight variations depending on wind conditions. Including taxiing, boarding and de‑icing procedures, the total gate‑to‑gate time is usually around two hours.

Which airlines fly from Newark to Montreal?

The airlines that operate flights between Newark (EWR) and Montreal (YUL) include United Airlines, Air Canada, JetBlue Airways and American Airlines, with United and Air Canada offering several daily non‑stop services. Delta Air Lines provides one‑stop connections via its US hubs, and WestJet runs seasonal direct flights in the summer. All carriers operate Economy cabins on this short‑haul route, and most also provide Premium Economy or Business options for a higher fare.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Newark to Montreal?

Yes, there are direct non‑stop flights from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Montreal‑Trudeau (YUL) operated by United Airlines, Air Canada and JetBlue Airways throughout the week. Which airports serve Montreal and Newark, and how far are they from the city centre?

Newark is served by Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), which has three terminals and lies about 6 km southwest of downtown Newark and roughly 16 km from Manhattan. Montreal is served by Montreal‑Trudeau International Airport (YUL), a single‑terminal facility located approximately 20 km east of downtown Montreal. Both airports are connected to their city centres by express bus, rail (AirTrain Newark and the 747 bus to Montreal’s central station) and taxi services, with travel times of 20‑30 minutes under normal traffic.

How many flights operate between Newark and Montreal, and what is the distance?

Around ten to twelve scheduled flights operate each day between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Montreal‑Trudeau (YUL), including both non‑stop services and one‑stop connections. The great‑circle distance between the two airports is 533 km (331 mi), which translates to a typical flight time of about 1 hour 30 minutes. Frequency varies by season, with more daily departures in the summer months when business and leisure demand peaks.

What is the best time of year to visit Montreal, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Montreal is from late May to early October, when the city enjoys warm temperatures, long daylight hours and numerous festivals such as the Jazz Festival and Just for Laughs. Average daytime highs range from 18 °C (64 °F) in May to 24 °C (75 °F) in July, while rainfall is moderate. Winter months are cold, with temperatures often below –5 °C (23 °F) and frequent snow, which can affect travel plans but also offers a festive atmosphere.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

JetBlue Airways is generally the cheapest airline on the Newark (EWR) to Montreal (YUL) route, offering basic Economy fares that are often lower than United or Air Canada during the off‑peak season. The trade‑off is a reduced baggage allowance, fewer complimentary services and a tighter seat pitch compared with legacy carriers. Passengers who prioritise price over extras may prefer JetBlue, while those seeking more inclusive amenities may choose United or Air Canada despite the higher fare.

What is the most convenient public transport option from Montreal‑Trudeau to downtown Old Montreal?

The most convenient public transport option from Montreal‑Trudeau International Airport (YUL) to downtown Old Montreal is the 747 Express Bus, which runs every 15‑20 minutes and drops passengers at the Central Station, a short metro ride (Orange line) to Place‑d’Armes station in Old Montreal. The total journey takes about 45‑60 minutes and costs CAD 10, offering a direct, wheelchair‑accessible link without the need for a taxi. Alternative options include a taxi (≈ 30 minutes) or rideshare services.
