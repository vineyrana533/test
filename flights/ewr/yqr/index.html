Travelling from Newark to Regina: what you need to know

  • Depart from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), the main international gateway for New Jersey and the New York metro area.
  • Arrive at Regina International Airport (YQR), the sole commercial airport serving Saskatchewan’s capital.
  • A nonstop flight would cover roughly 2 585 km in about 3 hours 30 minutes; most itineraries include a single stop and take 5‑8 hours total.
  • Regina operates on Central Standard Time (UTC‑6) year‑round, so the time difference is 1 hour behind Newark in winter and 2 hours during U.S. daylight‑saving time.
  • The EWR‑YQR pairing is the busiest on this corridor, even though most services are one‑stop.
  • Arrive at least two hours before departure for international checks; add an extra hour during peak summer travel.

How to find the cheapest flights from Newark to Regina

  • Book 6‑8 weeks ahead for the best mix of price and availability; last‑minute fares can jump 30‑50 %.
  • February is historically the cheapest month, with January and early March close behind.
  • Set up a price‑alert on a comparison site to be notified of drops; most alerts trigger within 24 hours of a fare change.
  • Use the “cheapest” or “shortest duration” filters and then compare the total travel time, as a lower price often means a longer layover.

Flying from Newark: Newark Liberty International Airport

  • No airline currently offers a nonstop service to Regina; all flights connect via a Canadian hub.
  • Common one‑stop connections are Toronto Pearson (YYZ), Calgary International (YYC) and Chicago O’Hare (ORD).
  • The airport sits about 16 km (10 mi) from downtown Newark; a car ride takes 20‑25 minutes depending on traffic.
  • NJ Transit’s Airport Express train reaches the terminals in roughly 30‑35 minutes, with a free shuttle to the terminals.

Arriving in Regina: Regina International Airport

  • YQR is located roughly 6 km (4 mi) east of Regina’s city centre; the drive normally takes 10‑15 minutes.
  • Regina Transit’s Route 30 bus links the terminal with downtown in about 20‑25 minutes; tickets can be bought on board.
  • Taxis and rideshare services are readily available outside the arrivals hall, with flat‑rate fares to the centre.
  • Once in the city, a car‑share or rental car is useful for reaching attractions spread across the metropolitan area.

Best time to go to Regina

  • July is the most popular month, coinciding with the Regina Folk Festival and warm weather.
  • For lower prices and fewer crowds, February offers off‑season rates and quiet streets.
  • Summer temperatures typically range from 18 °C to 26 °C, while winter averages –15 °C to –5 °C, with occasional sub‑zero wind chills.

More about Regina

  • The Royal Saskatchewan Museum showcases provincial natural history and Indigenous cultures.
  • Wascana Centre, a 930‑ha urban park surrounding Wascana Lake, provides walking trails, gardens and the Legislative Building.
  • The RCMP Heritage Centre offers interactive exhibits on Canada’s national police service.
  • Downtown’s historic streets feature boutique shops, art galleries and the vibrant 13th Avenue entertainment district.

Explore more of Saskatchewan

  • Saskatoon (≈260 km north) – attractions include the Meewasin Valley Trail, Wanuskewin Heritage Park and the vibrant Broadway Avenue.
  • Moose Jaw (≈260 km southwest) – famous for the Tunnels of Moose Jaw, the historic downtown and the Western Development Museum.
  • Swift Current (≈300 km southwest) – known for the Swift Current Museum, Riverhurst Lake and nearby prairie‑landscape parks.

Finding cheap flights from Newark to Regina: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Newark to Regina?

Search for the lowest fare by using flexible dates, checking both Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and nearby airports such as New York JFK, and setting price alerts on this page. Book 6‑8 weeks ahead of travel, especially for journeys in January, February or early November when demand is low. Compare itineraries that connect through Toronto Pearson (YYZ) or Chicago O'Hare (ORD), as these hubs often host the cheapest WestJet and Air Canada options.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Newark to Regina?

The cheapest way to travel from Newark (EWR) to Regina (YQR) is to book a connecting flight on a low‑cost carrier such as WestJet or Air Canada, depart in the off‑peak winter months, and keep travel dates flexible. Choosing early‑morning or late‑evening departures on Tuesday or Wednesday further reduces the price, while using price‑watch tools on this page helps you capture fare drops before they disappear.

When is the best time to book a flight from Newark to Regina to get the lowest price?

The optimal booking window for the lowest price on a Newark‑Regina itinerary is roughly 6‑8 weeks before the intended departure date. This period balances airline inventory release with the dip in demand that occurs after the initial booking surge. Booking during this window for travel in January, February or early November typically yields the deepest discounts, especially when combined with flexible date searches on this page.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Newark to Regina?

January, February and early November are the cheapest months to fly from Newark (EWR) to Regina (YQR). During these periods demand is low, airlines run promotional fares and the weather in both cities is off‑peak, which drives round‑trip prices down to the lower end of the typical range. Planning travel for these months maximises savings while still offering reasonable flight options.

How long is the flight from Newark to Regina?

A non‑stop sector between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Regina International Airport (YQR) would take about 4 hours 30 minutes of airborne time, covering roughly 2 585 km. Because there are no scheduled direct services, most itineraries include a connection in Toronto, Chicago or Minneapolis, adding 2‑4 hours of layover time and bringing total travel time to 6‑9 hours depending on the chosen routing.

Which airlines fly from Newark to Regina?

Airlines that operate connecting services between Newark (EWR) and Regina (YQR) include United Airlines, Air Canada, WestJet, American Airlines, Delta Air Lines and, on occasion, Alaska Airlines. These carriers typically route passengers through major hubs such as Toronto Pearson (YYZ), Chicago O'Hare (ORD) or Minneapolis‑St. Paul (MSP), providing multiple daily departure options across the week.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Newark to Regina?

No, there are currently no scheduled non‑stop flights between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Regina International Airport (YQR). All available itineraries require at least one connection, most commonly at Toronto Pearson (YYZ) or Chicago O'Hare (ORD), which adds transfer time but still delivers a total journey of under ten hours on most schedules.

How much does a flight from Newark to Regina usually cost and what drives the price?

Round‑trip fares between Newark (EWR) and Regina (YQR) typically range from US$350 in the low‑season winter months to around US$800 during the summer peak. Prices are driven by factors such as booking window, day of the week, carrier competition, and seasonal demand. Connecting through a major hub like Toronto often lowers the fare, while choosing a direct‑flight‑free itinerary with a low‑cost carrier can further reduce the cost.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Tuesday and Wednesday departures are generally the cheapest for the Newark‑Regina corridor, especially when flights leave early in the morning or late in the evening. Mid‑week travel avoids the higher business‑travel demand of Monday and Friday, and off‑peak flight times reduce seat‑fill pressure, resulting in lower published fares that can be captured through alerts on this page.

Which airports serve Regina and Newark, and how far are they from the city centre?

Regina International Airport (YQR) is located about 6 km north of downtown Regina and has a single terminal serving all domestic and limited international flights. Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) sits roughly 24 km southwest of Manhattan, New York City, and operates three terminals (A, B and C) that handle a mix of domestic and trans‑border services to Canada.

How many flights operate between Newark and Regina, and what is the distance?

Approximately 8‑10 scheduled connections per day operate between Newark (EWR) and Regina (YQR) across all airlines, covering a great‑circle distance of about 2 585 km. The frequency varies by season, with more options in the summer months and fewer in the winter, but the route remains well‑served through major Canadian and US hub airports.

What is the best time of year to visit Regina, and what is the weather like?

Late spring (May‑June) and early autumn (September) are the best times to visit Regina, offering mild daytime temperatures between 10 °C and 20 °C, low humidity and clear skies. Summer can be hot, often exceeding 30 °C, while winter brings sub‑zero temperatures and snow, making outdoor sightseeing less comfortable during those periods.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

WestJet usually provides the lowest fare on the Newark‑Regina route, especially when travelling in the off‑peak winter months. The trade‑off is that WestJet seats may be limited on popular dates and the airline offers a more basic onboard service compared with Air Canada, which includes complimentary meals on longer segments and a broader seat selection.

What passport or visa requirements apply for US citizens travelling from Newark to Regina?

US citizens travelling from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Regina International Airport (YQR) must present a valid US passport; no visa or electronic travel authorization is required for entry into Canada. Canadian citizens need a valid passport to enter the United States. Both countries allow short‑term visits without additional documentation, though travelers should verify any COVID‑19 or customs updates before departure.
