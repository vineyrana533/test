Travelling from Newark to Toronto: what you need to know

  • Depart from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), the main gateway for the New York metropolitan area, with three terminals.
  • Arrive at Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ), the busiest Canadian airport, which has two terminals.
  • A typical non‑stop flight covers about 558 km and lasts roughly 1 hour 30 minutes.
  • Both cities sit in the Eastern Time Zone (UTC‑5 in winter, UTC‑4 in summer), so there is no time‑difference to account for.
  • The EWR‑YYZ pairing is the busiest Canada‑US corridor for business and leisure travellers.
  • Aim to be at the departure hall at least two hours before an international flight, allowing extra time during peak holiday periods.

How to find the cheapest flights from Newark to Toronto

  • Set a price alert on a comparison site and monitor it for at least a month; fares often dip a few weeks before departure.
  • The sweet spot for booking is typically 6‑8 weeks ahead, when airlines release discounted inventory.
  • Historically, January and February offer the lowest average fares, especially mid‑week departures.
  • Use the “cheapest” or “fastest” sort filters, then narrow results by preferred cabin or airline to spot the best value.
  • Consider flexible dates; shifting a flight by one day can save 10‑20 % on the total cost.

Flying from Newark: Newark Liberty International Airport

  • United Airlines and Air Canada operate regular non‑stop services from EWR to YYZ.
  • Common one‑stop hubs for other carriers include Chicago O’Hare (ORD), Detroit Metropolitan (DTW) and Washington Dulles (IAD).
  • The airport lies about 24 km southwest of Manhattan; a car journey typically takes 30‑35 minutes, depending on traffic.
  • Public transport options: the AirTrain connects to NJ Transit and Amtrak; the combined trip to Midtown Manhattan averages 45‑50 minutes.
  • Parking is available both short‑term and long‑term, with rates varying by lot and duration.

Arriving in Toronto: Toronto Pearson International Airport

  • Pearson sits roughly 22 km northwest of downtown Toronto; a taxi or rideshare usually takes 25‑30 minutes, traffic permitting.
  • The UP Express train provides a direct 25‑minute link between YYZ and Union Station in the city centre.
  • TTC bus 192 runs between the airport and Kipling subway station; the total journey to the core is about 45‑50 minutes.
  • Once in the city, the Toronto Transit Commission (subway, streetcar and bus) offers extensive coverage, while GO Transit serves the Greater Toronto area.
  • Car rentals are available at all terminals; follow signs to the Rental Car Centre for a shuttle service.

Best time to go to Toronto

  • July is the most popular month, with festivals, warm weather and long daylight hours.
  • For a quieter, cheaper experience, aim for January or February when hotel rates drop and crowds thin.
  • Summer temperatures typically range from 20 °C to 27 °C, while winter averages hover between –5 °C and –1 °C, with occasional snowfall.
  • Spring (April‑May) and autumn (September‑October) offer mild weather and vibrant foliage, making them attractive shoulder‑season options.

More about Toronto

  • The iconic CN Tower rises 553 m above the skyline and features a glass‑floor observation deck.
  • The Royal Ontario Museum houses world‑class natural history and art collections.
  • The historic Distillery District showcases Victorian‑era industrial architecture, boutiques and eateries.
  • St. Lawrence Market is a culinary landmark offering fresh produce, local specialties and a bustling farmer’s atmosphere.
  • Toronto’s multicultural neighbourhoods – such as Kensington Market and Greektown – provide diverse dining and cultural experiences.

Explore more of Canada

  • Montreal, about 540 km east of Toronto, is famed for Old Montreal, the Notre‑Dame Basilica and its vibrant food scene.
  • Ottawa, roughly 350 km northeast, offers Parliament Hill, the Rideau Canal (a UNESCO World Heritage site) and national museums.
  • Niagara Falls, only 130 km south, draws visitors to the spectacular waterfalls, wine‑country tours and the Clifton Hill entertainment district.
  • Each city is reachable by GO Transit, VIA Rail or a short domestic flight, providing varied options for extended itineraries.

Finding cheap flights from Newark to Toronto: frequently asked questions

How do I find cheap flights from Newark to Toronto?

Search for flights on this page using flexible dates, set price alerts, and compare fares at least 6‑8 weeks before travel; the cheapest months are usually January, February and early November. Include nearby airports such as LaGuardia (LGA), JFK (JFK) or Buffalo (BUF) when checking outbound options, and consider both Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ). Booking mid‑week and travelling early morning or late evening often yields the lowest prices.

What is the cheapest way to fly from Newark to Toronto?

The cheapest way to fly from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) is to book a non‑stop economy ticket on United Airlines or Air Canada during off‑peak periods, using flexible dates and price‑alert tools on this page. Flying on a Tuesday or Wednesday, departing early in the morning, and avoiding major holidays typically reduces the fare to the lowest range of US$150‑200 round‑trip.

When is the best time to book a flight from Newark to Toronto to get the lowest price?

The optimal time to book a flight from Newark (EWR) to Toronto (YYZ) is 6‑8 weeks before departure, with a window of 2‑3 weeks offering the best chance of a discount if you monitor price alerts on this page. Prices tend to drop after the initial booking surge in September and rise again in late November for Thanksgiving travel, so early winter bookings are usually cheapest.

What is the cheapest month or time of year to fly from Newark to Toronto?

January, early February and the first half of November are historically the cheapest months to fly from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ). During these periods demand is low, airlines run promotional fares and the average fare falls 15‑25 % compared with summer or holiday peaks. Avoiding Canadian Thanksgiving in late October also helps keep costs down.

How long is the flight from Newark to Toronto?

A non‑stop flight from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) typically lasts about 1 hour 30 minutes, covering a distance of roughly 558 km. Slight variations occur due to wind and air traffic, but the scheduled block time is usually between 1 hour 25 minutes and 1 hour 35 minutes, making it one of the shortest international routes in North America.

Which airlines fly from Newark to Toronto?

Direct services between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) are operated by United Airlines and Air Canada. Both carriers offer multiple daily non‑stop flights, with United using its Boeing 737 fleet and Air Canada employing Airbus A320 family aircraft. Additional options with one stop include American Airlines, Delta Air Lines and WestJet, which connect through hubs such as Chicago (ORD) or Detroit (DTW).

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Newark to Toronto?

Yes, there are direct non‑stop flights between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) operated by United Airlines and Air Canada. These airlines provide several daily departures, typically three to four United flights and two Air Canada flights each day, allowing travellers to reach Toronto in about 1 hour 30 minutes without changing aircraft.

How much does a flight from Newark to Toronto usually cost and what drives the price?

A round‑trip economy ticket from Newark (EWR) to Toronto (YYZ) generally ranges from US$150 to US$350, depending on season, booking window and demand. Prices rise during Canadian Thanksgiving, US holidays and summer vacation months, while lower fares appear in January, early February and early November. Airline competition, fuel costs, and the choice of flexible versus fixed tickets also influence the final price, which can be monitored with alerts on this page.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Flying on Tuesday or Wednesday, especially on early‑morning departures before 9 am or late‑evening flights after 8 pm, usually yields the lowest fares on the Newark‑Toronto corridor. Mid‑week demand is weaker than on Monday or Friday, and airlines often release discounted seats for these off‑peak slots, making them the most cost‑effective choices for budget‑conscious travellers.

Which airports serve Toronto and Newark, and how far are they from the city centre?

Newark is served by Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), located about 16 km (10 miles) southwest of Manhattan and roughly 24 km (15 miles) from downtown Newark. Toronto is served by Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ), situated approximately 22 km (14 miles) northwest of downtown Toronto. Both airports have two main terminals—EWR with three terminals and YYZ with two—offering ground‑transport links such as trains, buses and rideshare services to their respective city centres.

How many flights operate between Newark and Toronto, and what is the distance?

On an average day, United Airlines operates three non‑stop flights and Air Canada operates two non‑stop flights between Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) and Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ), giving roughly five daily departures each way. The great‑circle distance between the two airports is about 558 km (347 miles), making it a short‑haul international route with frequent service.

What is the best time of year to visit Toronto, and what is the weather like?

The most pleasant time to visit Toronto is late May to early October, when temperatures range from 15 °C to 25 °C (59‑77 °F) and rainfall is moderate. Summer (June‑August) offers warm days and lively festivals, while early autumn provides comfortable weather and colourful foliage. Winter can be cold, with temperatures often below 0 °C (32 °F) and occasional snow, which may affect travel plans.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

United Airlines typically offers the lowest base fares on the Newark‑Toronto route, especially when booked early or during promotional periods. The trade‑off can be fewer complimentary services compared with Air Canada, such as limited free checked baggage on basic economy fares and fewer lounge access options. Passengers prioritising price over amenities often choose United, while those seeking a more inclusive experience may prefer Air Canada.

What is the easiest way to get from Toronto Pearson to downtown Toronto for a short layover?

The quickest way to travel from Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) to downtown Toronto during a short layover is the UP Express train, which departs every 15 minutes from Terminal 1 and reaches Union Station in 25 minutes. Tickets can be bought online or at the station, and the service runs from early morning until late night, providing a reliable and fast connection for business or leisure travellers.
