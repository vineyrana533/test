Travelling from Newark to Buenos Aires: what you need to know

  • Depart from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), the only commercial airport serving Newark, with three terminals.
  • Arrive at Ministro Pistarini International Airport (EZE), the main gateway to Buenos Aires, which has a single passenger terminal.
  • The average non‑stop flight covers 8 548 km and lasts roughly 11 hours 30 minutes.
  • Buenos Aires is two hours ahead of Newark (UTC‑3 versus UTC‑5 in winter, UTC‑4 in summer).
  • The busiest airport‑to‑airport pairing on this route is EWR ↔ EZE.
  • For an international departure, aim to be at the check‑in desk at least two hours before take‑off, allowing extra time during Argentine summer holidays.

How to find the cheapest flights from Newark to Buenos Aires

  • Book 8‑12 weeks in advance for the best chance of low fares; last‑minute deals are rare on this long‑haul route.
  • August and September are typically the cheapest months, coinciding with the Argentine winter low‑season.
  • Set up a price‑alert on a comparison site; the algorithm will notify you when fares dip below your target.
  • Use the “cheapest” filter first, then sort by total travel time to avoid hidden layover costs.
  • Flexible dates (mid‑week departures) usually shave 10‑20 % off the base price.

Flying from Newark: Newark Liberty International Airport

  • United Airlines runs the only regular non‑stop service to Buenos Aires; American, Delta and LATAM offer one‑stop options via Miami, Atlanta or São Paulo.
  • Common connection hubs include Miami International (MIA), Panama City (PTY) and São Paulo‑Guarulhos (GRU).
  • The airport lies about 24 km (15 miles) from downtown Newark; by car the journey takes 25‑35 minutes depending on traffic.
  • NJ Transit’s AirTrain links all three terminals to the Northeast Corridor rail line; a combined AirTrain‑train trip to Manhattan is roughly 45 minutes.

Arriving in Buenos Aires: Ministro Pistarini International Airport

  • EZE sits 22 km (14 miles) west of the city centre; a taxi or rideshare to downtown typically costs ARS 1 200‑1 500 and takes 30‑45 minutes.
  • The Aeroparque Buenos Aires (AEP) handles domestic traffic only and is not used for trans‑Atlantic arrivals.
  • Public transport options include the Airport Express bus (Line 8) to the city’s central bus terminal (Terminal C) in about 45 minutes, and a shuttle to the Mitre railway line for connections to suburbs.
  • Once in the city, the Subte (metro) and extensive bus network provide cheap, 24‑hour coverage.

Best time to go to Buenos Aires

  • The most popular month is December, when the city hosts festive celebrations and the weather is warm.
  • For lower prices and fewer crowds, travel in June or July, the Argentine winter, when temperatures range from 8 °C to 16 °C.
  • Summer (December‑February) sees averages of 20 °C‑30 °C, while winter (June‑August) stays between 5 °C‑15 °C.
  • Shoulder months (September‑October and March‑April) offer mild weather and moderate demand, balancing cost and comfort.

More about Buenos Aires

  • The historic neighbourhood of San Telmo is famed for its cobbled streets, tango venues and weekly antique market.
  • La Boca, with its colourful Caminito street and Boca Juniors stadium, showcases the city’s immigrant heritage.
  • Recoleta Cemetery houses the mausoleum of Eva Perón and is surrounded by upscale boutiques and cafés.
  • Palermo’s sprawling parks, trendy bars and the Botanical Garden make it a favourite for locals and visitors alike.

Explore more of Argentina

  • Córdoba, 700 km north‑west of Buenos Aires, is renowned for its colonial architecture and vibrant student life.
  • Mendoza, about 1 050 km west, lies at the foothills of the Andes and is the heart of Argentina’s wine country.
  • Rosario, 300 km north, is the birthplace of Che Guevara and offers riverfront promenades and modern art museums.
  • Each city is reachable by a 1‑2 hour domestic flight from EZE or by long‑distance bus services.

What is the best time of year to visit Buenos Aires, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Buenos Aires is during the Southern Hemisphere spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) when temperatures range from 15 °C to 25 °C and rainfall is moderate, making city tours and outdoor cafés pleasant. Spring brings blooming jacaranda trees that colour the streets purple, while autumn offers comfortable evenings for tango shows and museum visits.
