Travelling from Newark to Brussels: what you need to know

  • Depart from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), the only major airport serving the Newark area, with three terminals.
  • Arrive at Brussels Airport (BRU), the main gateway to Belgium, which has a single passenger terminal.
  • The average non‑stop flight time is around 7 hours 45 minutes, covering roughly 5 908 km.
  • Brussels is six hours ahead of Newark for most of the year (UTC+1 vs UTC‑5, shifting to five hours when both regions observe daylight saving).
  • The busiest airport‑to‑airport pairing on this route is EWR ↔ BRU.
  • For an international departure, aim to be at the terminal at least two hours before take‑off, allowing extra time during summer holidays and major festivals.

How to find the cheapest flights from Newark to Brussels

  • Book 8‑12 weeks in advance for the best balance of price and seat availability; ultra‑early booking (four months ahead) can yield lower fares during off‑peak months.
  • January and February are typically the cheapest months to fly, with lower demand after the New Year holidays.
  • Set a price‑alert on a comparison site; the tool will email you when fares drop below your target.
  • When sorting results, use the “cheapest” filter first, then refine by “shortest duration” to avoid long layovers.
  • Mid‑week departures, especially Tuesdays and Wednesdays, often cost less than weekend flights.

Flying from Newark: Newark Liberty International Airport

  • Brussels Airlines operates non‑stop services; United Airlines offers codeshare flights on the same schedule.
  • Common one‑stop connections are via Frankfurt (FRA) with Lufthansa, Paris Charles‑de‑Gaulle (CDG) with Air France, and Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) with KLM.
  • The airport lies about 24 km (15 miles) from downtown Manhattan and 16 km (10 miles) from Newark’s city centre.
  • By car or rideshare, the journey to the terminals takes roughly 30 minutes in moderate traffic; the AirTrain + NJ Transit train to Penn Station is about 45 minutes total.

Arriving in Brussels: Brussels Airport

  • The airport is situated 12 km (7.5 miles) north‑east of Brussels city centre.
  • A direct train (Brussels Airport – Zaventem) runs every 15 minutes, taking 20 minutes to reach the central station.
  • Buses (line 12) and taxis are also available; a taxi ride to the city typically costs €45‑€55 and lasts 20‑25 minutes depending on traffic.
  • Once in the city, the extensive tram and metro network provides easy access to historic districts, museums and the European Quarter.

Best time to go to Brussels

  • July and August see the highest visitor numbers, driven by festivals such as the Brussels Summer Festival.
  • For quieter streets and lower accommodation rates, visit in March or November.
  • Summer temperatures average 18‑24 °C, while winter lows hover around 0‑4 °C, with occasional frost.
  • Spring (April‑May) offers mild weather (10‑16 °C) and the city’s famous flower carpets in the Grand‑Place.

More about Brussels

  • The Grand‑Place, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is renowned for its ornate guildhalls and the Town Hall.
  • Atomium, the iconic steel structure from the 1958 World’s Fair, offers panoramic city views.
  • The Royal Museum of Fine Arts houses Flemish masterpieces, while the European Parliament complex showcases modern architecture.
  • Sample local specialties such as mussels with fries, chocolate pralines and a wide range of Belgian beers in the historic neighbourhoods of Sablon and Saint‑Gilles.

Explore more of Belgium

  • Antwerp, about 45 km (28 miles) north of Brussels, is famed for its diamond district and the Cathedral of Our Lady, home to Rubens’ works.
  • Bruges, 100 km (62 miles) north‑west, offers medieval canals, the Markt square and the Belfry, a UNESCO site.
  • Ghent, roughly 55 km (34 miles) west, combines historic architecture with a vibrant student scene; highlights include Gravensteen castle and St Bavo’s Cathedral.

United Airlines, Brussels Airlines, Lufthansa, British Airways and Air Canada all operate services between Newark and Brussels, either as direct flights or with a single European connection. Which airports serve Newark and Brussels, and how far are they from the city centre?

Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) lies about six kilometres southwest of downtown Newark and roughly 16 km from Manhattan, with easy access via NJ Transit and the AirTrain. Brussels Airport (BRU) is situated approximately 12 km north of Brussels city centre and is linked by a 20‑minute train service (Brussels Airport Express) that runs every 15 minutes. How many flights operate between Newark and Brussels, and what is the distance?

About five to seven non‑stop flights operate each week between Newark and Brussels, supplemented by several daily one‑stop services, giving a total of roughly 20‑25 weekly departures. What is the best time of year to visit Brussels, and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Brussels is late spring (April to June) when temperatures average 12‑20 °C and the city’s gardens are in bloom, or early autumn (September to October) for mild weather and fewer tourists. Summer can be warm with occasional rain, while winter is chilly, with temperatures often around 0‑5 °C and occasional snow in the city.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route, and what is the trade‑off?

Brussels Airlines often offers the lowest base fare on the Newark‑Brussels route, especially when you travel in the off‑peak months and book well in advance. The trade‑off is that its economy tickets may have stricter change fees, limited free checked baggage and fewer premium cabin options compared with United’s more flexible fare classes for business travellers.

What major European hub can I use to connect between Newark and Brussels for a cheaper fare?

If you are willing to connect, flying via Frankfurt Airport (FRA) or London Heathrow (LHR) often yields cheaper fares than a direct Newark‑Brussels service. Both hubs are served by United, Lufthansa and British Airways from Newark, with onward flights to Brussels in under two hours. Connecting in these airports also provides more flight time options and the chance to collect frequent‑flyer miles on multiple carriers.
