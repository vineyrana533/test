Flying from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta

Flying from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta

The distance between Baltimore/Washington International Airport (BWI) and Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR) is approximately 3,413 km (2,119 mi). There are no nonstop services; the most common itineraries involve a single stop in Houston (IAH), Dallas (DFW), Miami (MIA) or Mexico City (MEX). Total travel time, including layover, averages 9–11 hours, with the in‑air segment lasting about 7–8 hours.

Baltimore/Washington International Airport (BWI)

Baltimore/Washington International Airport (BWI)

BWI is a single‑terminal airport with 3 concourses and a single departure gate area. It is located about 12 km (7 mi) north of downtown Baltimore and 35 km (22 mi) south of Washington, D.C. Ground transport options include Amtrak Penn Line to Baltimore Penn Station, the MARC Train, bus services such as the 60‑line, taxis, rideshares and rental cars. Parking is available in the long‑term, short‑term and valet lots, and the airport is served by the I‑95 corridor, making it accessible from both cities.

Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR)

Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR)

PVR sits on a former military airfield 30 km (18 mi) south of Puerto Vallarta’s downtown, on the Pacific coast. It has a single terminal with one concourse and a handful of gates. Ground transport to the city centre includes local buses (Line 1), taxis, rideshares, and a 15‑minute shuttle service operated by the airport. Rental car facilities are available on‑site, and the airport is connected to the I‑15 highway for easy access to nearby towns such as Sayulita and San Sebastián del Oeste.

Finding the cheapest fares

Finding the cheapest fares

Because there are no nonstop flights, the cheapest fares are usually found on one‑stop itineraries operated by United, American, Delta, LATAM or Aeromexico. Book 6–8 weeks in advance and set price alerts on a multi‑airline search engine. Flexible dates help; mid‑week and early‑morning or late‑night departures tend to be cheaper. Compare nearby US airports such as Washington Dulles (IAD) or Reagan National (DCA) if they offer lower fares for the same route. Avoid peak‑season months (December–March) to keep costs down.

Best time of year to travel

Best time of year to travel

Puerto Vallarta’s dry season runs from November to April, offering sunny weather and lower humidity – ideal for beach activities. Prices for flights and accommodation rise during this period. The low‑season from May to October sees higher rainfall and humidity, but fares drop and crowds thin. Early May and September are the cheapest months for flights, while November and February provide a balance of good weather and moderate prices. Seasonal festivals such as the Guelaguetza in July can also impact availability and cost.

Getting around in Puerto Vallarta

Getting around in Puerto Vallarta

Once in Puerto Vallarta, the city’s compact layout makes walking a pleasant option for exploring Old Town, the Malecon boardwalk and the marina. For longer distances, local buses (Line 1) and taxis are plentiful. A 15‑minute airport shuttle takes you into town, and several companies offer day‑trip tours to nearby beaches like Sayulita and San Sebastián del Oeste. Renting a car is convenient for exploring the Sierra Madre foothills, but traffic in the city centre can be heavy during peak hours.

Finding cheap flights from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta: frequently asked questions

How long is the flight from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta?

A direct flight from Baltimore/Washington International Airport (BWI) to Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR) would take roughly 6 hours and 30 minutes, covering the 3,413‑km distance. In practice, most itineraries involve a layover in a US hub such as Dallas, Los Angeles or Phoenix, so total travel time including connections is usually between 9 and 12 hours.

Which airlines fly from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta?

The main carriers operating routes between BWI and PVR are United Airlines, American Airlines, Delta Air Lines and Alaska Airlines. Low‑cost airlines such as Frontier and Spirit also offer flights with a stop‑over in a US hub. These airlines provide the most frequent service and a range of fare options for this trans‑Atlantic route.

Are there direct (non‑stop) flights from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta?

No, there are currently no non‑stop flights from BWI to PVR. All itineraries require at least one stop‑over in a US hub such as Dallas/Fort Worth, Los Angeles or Phoenix before connecting to a flight to Puerto Vallarta.

How much does a flight from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta cost and what drives the price?

Prices for a round‑trip ticket from BWI to PVR typically range from £200 to £450, depending on the airline, time of year and how far in advance it is booked. Factors that drive cost include seasonality, demand, the length of the layover, and whether the fare is a basic economy or a full‑service ticket. Low‑cost carriers usually offer the lowest base fares but add fees for baggage and seat selection.

What is the cheapest month or time to fly from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta?

The cheapest period to fly from BWI to PVR is usually the low‑season months of January, February, September and October. During these times airlines reduce fares to attract travellers, and the weather in Puerto Vallarta is still pleasant, with lower humidity than the peak summer months.

What is the best day of the week and time of day to fly this route for a lower fare?

Mid‑week departures, particularly Tuesdays and Wednesdays, tend to offer the lowest fares for the BWI‑PVR route. Early‑morning flights (before 9 am) are often cheaper than late‑afternoon or evening departures, as airlines price these times to fill seats that are less convenient for many passengers.

How far in advance should I book flights from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta?

Booking 2 to 3 months in advance is usually the sweet spot for the BWI‑PVR route. This window balances fare competitiveness with flexibility, and it gives you a good chance to secure a convenient layover time and a seat on a preferred airline.

Which airports serve Puerto Vallarta and Baltimore, and how far are they from the city centre?

Baltimore is served by Baltimore/Washington International Airport (BWI), located about 20 km (12 mi) north of downtown Baltimore. Puerto Vallarta is served by Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR), situated roughly 5 km (3 mi) north of the city centre. Both airports have public transport links, taxis and rideshare options that reach the city in 15–30 minutes depending on traffic.

How many flights operate between Baltimore and Puerto Vallarta?

There are typically 2 to 3 daily flight options between BWI and PVR when including connecting flights. The frequency varies by season, with peak summer months seeing more departures and winter months offering fewer. Airlines adjust schedules to match demand, so checking a few days in advance can reveal the best times.

What is the flight distance between Baltimore and Puerto Vallarta?

The great‑circle distance between Baltimore/Washington International Airport (BWI) and Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR) is approximately 3,413 km (2,119 mi). This figure represents the shortest path over the earth’s surface and is used by airlines to calculate fuel needs and flight duration.

What is the best time of year to visit Puerto Vallarta and what is the weather like?

The best time to visit Puerto Vallarta is from November to April, when the weather is dry, sunny and temperatures range from 20 °C to 27 °C (68 °F to 81 °F). This period avoids the rainy season that runs from June to October, when afternoon showers and higher humidity are common.

How do I get the cheapest flight from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta?

To secure the cheapest flight from BWI to PVR, compare fares on low‑cost carriers such as Frontier and Spirit, book 2–3 months ahead, and choose mid‑week, early‑morning departures. Use fare‑alert tools, consider flexible travel dates, and book a basic economy ticket while adding paid baggage only if necessary to keep the base fare low.

Which is the cheapest airline on this route and what is the trade‑off?

Frontier Airlines often offers the lowest base fares on the BWI‑PVR route, but the trade‑off includes a strict baggage policy, no free seat selection, and additional fees for in‑flight food and entertainment. If you value comfort and convenience, a full‑service carrier like United or American may be worth the higher price.

What major hub in the United States connects flights from Baltimore to Puerto Vallarta?

Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) is a key connecting hub for passengers travelling from BWI to PVR. Many airlines, including United and American, use DFW as a transfer point, offering multiple daily flights to Puerto Vallarta and convenient connections from Baltimore.
